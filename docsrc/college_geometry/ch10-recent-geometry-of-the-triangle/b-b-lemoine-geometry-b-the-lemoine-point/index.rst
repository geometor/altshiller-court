Section B.b — Lemoine Geometry: The Lemoine Point
=================================================

.. rubric:: Chapter X: Recent Geometry of the Triangle • Articles §§ 572–587 (Folios 0271–0276, pp. 252–257)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Triangle ABC with symmedians meeting at Lemoine point K, parallels through K cutting the sides in six concyclic points on the First Lemoine Circle, and Brocard circle on diameter OK
   :caption: Symmedians, Lemoine Point K = X(6), First Lemoine Six-Point Circle, and Brocard Circle

Establishes the concurrency of the three symmedians at the :term:`Lemoine (symmedian) point <lemoine-point>` :math:`K = X(6)` (**§§ 572–587**, :person:`Grebe <grebe>` 1847; :cite:`Lemoine 1873 <lemoine:1873>`), the crown jewel of late-nineteenth-century triangle geometry.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 572–578 — Concurrency & Gergonne Identification**: Ceva's theorem on :math:`(c^2/b^2)(a^2/c^2)(b^2/a^2) = 1` proves concurrency at :math:`K`, whose distances to :math:`BC, CA, AB` are proportional to :math:`a, b, c`; :math:`K` is the :term:`Gergonne point <gergonne-point>` of the tangential triangle!
* **§§ 579–587 — Gauss–Lemoine Least-Squares Minimum & Centroid Pedal Property**: Among all points in the plane, :math:`K = X(6)` uniquely minimizes the sum of squares of distances :math:`d_a^2 + d_b^2 + d_c^2` to the three sides (**§ 581**), and :math:`K` is the unique point that is the centroid of its own pedal triangle!

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

Constructed with exact rational coordinates :math:`K = (a^2 A + b^2 B + c^2 C)/(a^2 + b^2 + c^2)` in ``geometor/etc`` (:math:`X(6)`) and the ``lemoine_symmedian`` hero figure.
