Section F — Tucker Circles (incl. Taylor Circle § 689)
======================================================

.. rubric:: Chapter X: Recent Geometry of the Triangle • Articles §§ 684–689 (Folios 0303–0306, pp. 284–287)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Triangle ABC with symmedians meeting at Lemoine point K, parallels through K cutting the sides in six concyclic points on the First Lemoine Circle, and Brocard circle on diameter OK
   :caption: Symmedians, Lemoine Point K = X(6), First Lemoine Six-Point Circle, and Brocard Circle

Generalizes the circumcircle, first and second Lemoine circles, and three-point pedal circles into the one-parameter family of :term:`Tucker circles <tucker-circles>` (**§§ 684–689**, :person:`Robert Tucker <tucker>` 1883), highlighted by the :person:`Taylor <taylor>` circle (**§ 689**).

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 684–688 — Six-Point Antiparallel-Parallel Hexagon**: If a triangle homothetic to :math:`\triangle ABC` from the :term:`Lemoine point <lemoine-point>` :math:`K` has its sides extended to meet :math:`BC, CA, AB`, the six resulting intercepts have alternating parallel and equal antiparallel chords and lie on a **Tucker circle** whose center lies on segment :math:`OK`!
* **§ 689 — The Taylor Circle**: The six orthogonal projections of the altitude feet :math:`D, E, F` onto the other two sides of :math:`\triangle ABC` lie on a distinguished Tucker circle discovered by :person:`H. M. Taylor <taylor>` (centered at :math:`X(389)`).

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

Provides a continuous affine family parameterized along the Brocard axis :math:`OK` in ``geometor/etc``.
