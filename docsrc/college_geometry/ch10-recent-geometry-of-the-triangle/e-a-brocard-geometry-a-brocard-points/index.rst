Section E.a — Brocard Geometry: Brocard Points
==============================================

.. rubric:: Chapter X: Recent Geometry of the Triangle • Articles §§ 649–665 (Folios 0293–0298, pp. 274–279)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Triangle ABC with symmedians meeting at Lemoine point K, parallels through K cutting the sides in six concyclic points on the First Lemoine Circle, and Brocard circle on diameter OK
   :caption: Symmedians, Lemoine Point K = X(6), First Lemoine Six-Point Circle, and Brocard Circle

Introduces the asymmetric rotational jewel of triangle geometry (**§§ 649–665**): the direct and retrograde :term:`Brocard points <brocard-points>` :math:`\Omega, \Omega'` and the Brocard angle :math:`\omega` (:person:`Crelle <crelle>` 1816, :person:`Jacobi <jacobi>` 1828, :cite:`Brocard 1881 <brocard:1881>`).

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 649–655 — Construction via Tangent Adjunct Circles**: Three circles tangent to :math:`BC, CA, AB` at :math:`B, C, A` and passing through :math:`C, A, B` concur at the first Brocard point :math:`\Omega` where :math:`\angle\Omega BC = \angle\Omega CA = \angle\Omega AB = \omega`; cyclic reversal yields :math:`\Omega'`, the :term:`isogonal conjugate <isogonal-conjugates>` of :math:`\Omega`!
* **§§ 656–665 — Brocard Angle Formula & Inequality**: Proves :math:`\cot\omega = \cot A + \cot B + \cot C = (a^2 + b^2 + c^2) / 4S`, :math:`\csc^2\omega = \csc^2 A + \csc^2 B + \csc^2 C`, and :math:`\omega \le 30^\circ` (equality iff :math:`\triangle ABC` is equilateral).

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

Notice :math:`\cot\omega = (Q_a + Q_b + Q_c) / 4S` is rational in the side quadrances and area, connecting Brocard geometry to ``geometor/etc`` (:math:`X(39)` Brocard midpoint) and ``geometor/wildberger``.
