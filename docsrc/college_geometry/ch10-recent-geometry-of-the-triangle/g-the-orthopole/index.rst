Section G — The Orthopole
=========================

.. rubric:: Chapter X: Recent Geometry of the Triangle • Articles §§ 690–699 (Folios 0306–0310, pp. 287–291)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Triangle ABC with symmedians meeting at Lemoine point K, parallels through K cutting the sides in six concyclic points on the First Lemoine Circle, and Brocard circle on diameter OK
   :caption: Symmedians, Lemoine Point K = X(6), First Lemoine Six-Point Circle, and Brocard Circle

Culminates the entire 699-article treatise with :person:`Joseph Neuberg's <neuberg>` theory of the :term:`orthopole <orthopole>` :math:`M` of a line :math:`m` with respect to :math:`\triangle ABC` (**§§ 690–699**, :cite:`Gallatly 1913 <gy>`).

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 690–694 — Existence & Concurrency**: Drop perpendiculars :math:`AA_1, BB_1, CC_1` from the vertices of :math:`\triangle ABC` onto any line :math:`m`; the perpendiculars from :math:`A_1, B_1, C_1` onto the opposite sides :math:`BC, CA, AB` concur at the :term:`orthopole <orthopole>` :math:`M` of :math:`m`!
* **§§ 695–699 — Circumdiameter, Simson & Pedal-Circle Laws**: If :math:`m` passes through the circumcenter :math:`O`, its orthopole lies on the :term:`nine-point circle <nine-point-circle>` (and equals the Simson-line intersection of the diameter endpoints!); if :math:`m` cuts :math:`(O)` in :math:`P, Q`, then :math:`M` is the intersection of the :term:`Simson lines <simson-line>` of :math:`P` and :math:`Q` (Soons, :person:`Neuberg <neuberg>`, Lemoyne)!

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

Synthesizes orthocenter, nine-point, Simson-line, and pedal-circle geometry into one unified incidence theorem verifiable in ``geometor/model``.
