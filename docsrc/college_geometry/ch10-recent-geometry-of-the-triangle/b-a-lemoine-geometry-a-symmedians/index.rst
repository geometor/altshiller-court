Section B.a — Lemoine Geometry: Symmedians
==========================================

.. rubric:: Chapter X: Recent Geometry of the Triangle • Articles §§ 558–571 (Folios 0266–0271, pp. 247–252)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Triangle ABC with symmedians meeting at Lemoine point K, parallels through K cutting the sides in six concyclic points on the First Lemoine Circle, and Brocard circle on diameter OK
   :caption: Symmedians, Lemoine Point K = X(6), First Lemoine Six-Point Circle, and Brocard Circle

Develops the theory of :term:`symmedians <symmedian>` (**§§ 558–571**, :person:`Maurice d'Ocagne <docagne>`, :person:`Émile Lemoine <lemoine>`): the reflections of the medians of a triangle across the corresponding internal angle bisectors.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 558–564 — Antiparallel Bisection & Harmonic Tangents**: A symmedian from :math:`A` bisects every antiparallel to :math:`BC`, passes through the pole of :math:`BC` with respect to the :term:`circumcircle <circumcircle>` (intersection of tangents at :math:`B` and :math:`C`), and divides :math:`BC` in the squared side ratio :math:`c^2 : b^2`!
* **§§ 565–571 — Distances Proportional to Sides**: Points on the :math:`A`-symmedian have perpendicular distances to sides :math:`AB, AC` proportional to the side lengths :math:`c : b`.

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

Because symmedians divide opposite sides in squared ratios :math:`c^2 : b^2` (*quadrances*!), symmedian geometry is 100% rational over :math:`\mathbb{Q}(A, B, C)` in ``geometor/wildberger``.
