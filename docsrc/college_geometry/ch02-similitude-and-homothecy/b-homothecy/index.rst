Section B — Homothecy
=====================

.. rubric:: Chapter II: Similitude and Homothecy • Articles §§ 31–53 (Folios 0057–0071, pp. 38–52)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Two unequal circles O, O' with parallel radii, external center of similitude S, internal center S', and dashed circle of similitude
   :caption: Direct and Inverse Centers of Similitude S, S' and Circle of Similitude

Develops central dilation or :term:`homothecy <homothecy>` :math:`\mathcal{H}(S, k)` (**§§ 31–53**) as a point-to-point geometric transformation preserving parallelism, angles, and ratio of lengths, culminating in the :term:`centers of similitude <centers-of-similitude>` and circle of similitude of two circles.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 31–38 — Homothetic Figures & Homothetic Center**: Homologous segments are parallel and proportional (:math:`S P' / S P = k`); any two unequal parallel segments or circles are both directly (:math:`k > 0`) and inversely (:math:`k < 0`) homothetic.
* **§§ 39–45 — Direct & Inverse Centers of Similitude of Two Circles**: Two unequal circles :math:`(O, R)` and :math:`(O', R')` possess external and internal centers of similitude :math:`S, S'` dividing :math:`OO'` :term:`harmonically <harmonic-division>` in ratio :math:`R : R'`, through which common tangents and antihomologous secants pass.
* **§§ 46–53 — Circle of Similitude & The Pantograph**: Proves that the locus of points from which two unequal circles subtend equal angles is the :term:`Apollonian circle <apollonian-circle>` on diameter :math:`SS'`, and analyzes Scheiner's mechanical pantograph linkage.

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

Applied to a triangle's circumcircle and incircle in ``geometor/etc``, the internal and external similicenters yield Kimberling centers :math:`X(55)` and :math:`X(56)`.
