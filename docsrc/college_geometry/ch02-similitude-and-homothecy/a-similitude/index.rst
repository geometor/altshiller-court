Section A — Similitude
======================

.. rubric:: Chapter II: Similitude and Homothecy • Articles §§ 23–30 (Folios 0053–0057, pp. 34–38)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Triangle ABC with prototype dashed square P0 Q0 R0 S0 scaled by ray BQ0 into inscribed square PQRS
   :caption: Method of Similitude: Prototype Square Scaled from Homothetic Center B into △ABC

Introduces the constructive :term:`method of similitude <similitude>` (**§§ 23–30**): when all conditions save one linear dimension determine the shape (*species*) of a figure, a convenient similar prototype is constructed first and then scaled to satisfy the remaining boundary or metric condition (:cite:`Euclid VI <euclid:elements>`).

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 23–25 — Principle of Auxiliary Similar Prototypes**: Suppressing one linear datum converts a rigid placement problem into a one-parameter family of similar figures sharing fixed angular and ratio invariants.
* **§§ 26–28 — Inscribed Squares & Given-Shape Triangles**: Classic constructions inscribing a square in a given triangle :math:`ABC` and inscribing a triangle of given angles or side ratios inside another triangle or circle.
* **§§ 29–30 — Ratio-of-Distances Loci & Exercises**: Reduces linear combinations of distances from two intersecting lines to straight-line loci passing through their vertex.

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

Models scaling transformations in ``geometor/fundamentals`` and ``geometor/elements`` Book VI, where rational scale factors preserve exact algebraic field membership.
