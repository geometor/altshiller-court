Harmonic Ranges, Pencils, and Complete Quadrangles
==================================================

.. rubric:: Chapter VII: Harmonic Division • Articles §§ 345–357 (Folios 0185–0190, pp. 166–171)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Complete quadrangle KLMN whose sides meet line AB in harmonic conjugates A, C, B, D
   :caption: Complete Quadrangle KLMN Generating Harmonic Range (A, B; C, D) = −1 by Straightedge Alone

Elevates :term:`harmonic division <harmonic-division>` (**§§ 345–357**, Folios ``0185``–``0190``, pp. ``166``–``171``) from a metric segment ratio to the fundamental projective invariant of harmonic pencils, complete quadrangles, and complete quadrilaterals (:cite:`Pappus VII <pappus:collection:VII>`, :cite:`Chasles <chl>`).

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 345–349 — Algebraic & Midpoint Characterizations**: Proves the equivalence of :math:`(A,B;C,D) = -1` with the harmonic mean :math:`\frac{2}{AB} = \frac{1}{AC} + \frac{1}{AD}` and :person:`Newton's <newton>` midpoint relation :math:`MA^2 = MB^2 = MC \cdot MD`.
* **§§ 350–353 — Harmonic Pencils & Gauss–Newton Line**: Any transversal cuts a harmonic pencil :math:`O(ABCD)` in four harmonic points; a transversal parallel to one ray is bisected by the other three; midpoints of the three diagonals of a :term:`complete quadrilateral <complete-quadrilateral>` are collinear (:person:`Gauss <gauss>`–:person:`Newton <newton>` line).
* **§§ 354–357 — Complete Quadrangle & Straightedge Harmonic Construction**: Opposite sides of a complete quadrangle meet a diagonal line in harmonic conjugates, constructing the fourth harmonic point with the straightedge alone!

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

Directly unites ``geometor/pappus`` (projective cross-ratio invariance) and ``geometor/divine`` (automated detection of harmonic and golden ranges), illustrated in the ``harmonic_quadrangle`` hero figure.
