Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Supplementary Exercise 15 reduces the perimeter of trapezoid :math:`BB'C'C` to an indirect sum along the sidelines :math:`AB, AC`, while Exercise 16 constructs a triangle whose sides are internally divided in given ratios by three fixed points (a classic affine-dilation / parallel-translation problem). Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Item 17 (:math:`a - b, h_b + h_c, A`) pairs the *datum* :math:`\{b + c, h_b + h_c, A\}` of § 21 (yielding :math:`b + c`) with :math:`a - b` to give :math:`a + c = (a - b) + (b + c)`, reducing the triangle to two linear conditions and an angle.
