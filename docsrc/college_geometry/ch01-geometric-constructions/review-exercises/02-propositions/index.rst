Propositions (28–58)
====================

.. folio:: 0049
   :page: 30

28. The circle through the vertices :math:`A, B, C` of a parallelogram :math:`ABCD` meets :math:`DA, DC` in the points :math:`A', C'`. Prove that :math:`A'D : A'C' = A'C : A'B`.
29. Of the three lines joining the vertices of an equilateral triangle to a point on its :term:`circumcircle`, one is equal to the sum of the other two.
30. Three parallel lines drawn through the vertices of a triangle :math:`ABC` meet the respectively opposite sides in the points :math:`X, Y, Z`. Show that:

    .. math::

       \operatorname{area} XYZ : \operatorname{area} ABC = 2 : 1.

31. If the distance between two points is equal to the sum (or the difference) of the tangents from these points to a given circle, show that the line joining the two points is tangent to the circle.
32. Two parallel lines :math:`AE, BD` through the vertices :math:`A, B` of the triangle :math:`ABC` meet a line through the vertex :math:`C` in the points :math:`E, D`. If the parallel through :math:`E` to :math:`BC` meets :math:`AB` in :math:`F`, show that :math:`DF` is parallel to :math:`AC`.

.. folio:: 0050
   :page: 31

33. A variable chord :math:`AB` of a given circle is parallel to a fixed diameter passing through a given point :math:`P`. Show that the sum of the squares of the distances of :math:`P` from the ends of :math:`AB` is constant and equal to twice the square of the distance of :math:`P` from the midpoint of the arc :math:`AB`.
34. The points :math:`A', B', C'` divide the sides :math:`BC, CA, AB` of the triangle :math:`ABC` internally in the same ratio, :math:`k`. Show that the three triangles :math:`AB'C', BC'A', CA'B'` are equivalent, and find the ratio of the areas :math:`ABC, A'B'C'`.
35. The sides :math:`BA, CD` of the quadrilateral :math:`ABCD` meet in :math:`O`, and the sides :math:`DA, CB` meet in :math:`O'`. Along :math:`OA, OC, O'A, O'C` are measured off, respectively, :math:`OE, OF, O'E', O'F'` equal to :math:`AB, DC, AD, BC`. Prove that :math:`EF` is parallel to :math:`E'F'`.
36. The point :math:`P` of a circle, center :math:`O`, is projected into :math:`N` upon a diameter :math:`AOB`. Along :math:`PO` lay off :math:`PQ = 2\,AN`. If :math:`AQ` meets the circle again in :math:`R`, prove that angle :math:`AOR = 3\,AOP`.
37. If :math:`P` is any point on a semicircle, diameter :math:`AB`, and :math:`BC, CD` are two equal arcs, then if :math:`E = (CA, PB)`, :math:`F = (AD, PC)`, prove that :math:`AD` is perpendicular to :math:`EF`.
38. In the triangle :math:`ODE` the side :math:`OD` is smaller than :math:`OE` and :math:`O` is a right angle. :math:`A, B` are two points on the hypotenuse :math:`DE` such that angle :math:`AOD = BOD = 45^\circ`. Show that the line :math:`MO` joining :math:`O` to the midpoint :math:`M` of :math:`DE` is tangent to the circle :math:`OAB`.
39. From the point :math:`S` the two tangents :math:`SA, SB` and the secant :math:`SPQ` are drawn to the same circle. Prove that :math:`AP : AQ = BP : BQ`.
40. On the radius :math:`OA`, produced, take any point :math:`P` and draw a tangent :math:`PT`; produce :math:`OP` to :math:`Q`, making :math:`PQ = PT`, and draw a tangent :math:`QV`; if :math:`VR` be drawn perpendicular to :math:`OA`, meeting :math:`OA` at :math:`R`, prove that :math:`PR = PQ = PT`.
41. The parallel to the side :math:`AC` through the vertex :math:`B` of the triangle :math:`ABC` meets the tangent to the :term:`circumcircle` :math:`(O)` of :math:`ABC` at :math:`C` in :math:`B'`, and the parallel through :math:`C` to :math:`AB` meets the tangent to :math:`(O)` at :math:`B` in :math:`C'`. Prove that :math:`BC^2 = BC' \cdot B'C`.
42. Two variable transversals :math:`PQ, P'Q'` determine on two fixed lines :math:`OPP', OQQ'` two segments :math:`PP', QQ'` of fixed lengths. If :math:`L, M` are two points on :math:`PQ, P'Q'` such that :math:`PL : LQ = P'M : MQ' = \text{a constant ratio}`, prove that :math:`LM` is fixed in magnitude and direction.
43. If :math:`Q, R` are the projections of a point :math:`M` of the internal bisector :math:`AM` of the angle :math:`A` of the triangle :math:`ABC` upon the sides :math:`AC, AB`, show that the perpendicular :math:`MP` from :math:`M` upon :math:`BC` meets :math:`QR` in the point :math:`N` on the median :math:`AA'` of :math:`ABC`.
44. A circle touching :math:`AB` at :math:`B` and passing through the :term:`incenter <incircle>` :math:`I` (i.e., the center :math:`I` of the inscribed circle) of the triangle :math:`ABC` meets :math:`AC` in :math:`H, K`. Prove that :math:`IC` bisects the angle :math:`HIK`.
45. :math:`AB, CD` are two chords of the same circle, and the lines joining :math:`A, B` to the midpoint of :math:`CD` make equal angles with :math:`CD`. Show that the lines joining :math:`C, D` to the midpoint of :math:`AB` make equal angles with :math:`AB`.
46. Three pairs of circles :math:`(B), (C)`; :math:`(C), (A)`; :math:`(A), (B)` touch each other in :math:`D, E, F`. The lines :math:`DE, DF` meet the circle :math:`(A)` again in the points :math:`G, H`. Show that :math:`GH` passes through the center of :math:`(A)` and is parallel to the line of centers of the circles :math:`(B)` and :math:`(C)`.
47. The :term:`mediators <mediator>` of the sides :math:`AC, AB` of the triangle :math:`ABC` meet the sides :math:`AB, AC` in :math:`P, Q`. Prove that the points :math:`B, C, P, Q` lie on a circle which passes through the :term:`circumcenter <circumcircle>` (i.e., the center of the circumscribed circle) of :math:`ABC`.

48. :math:`MNP, M'N'P'` are two tangents to the same circle :math:`PQP'`, and :math:`AM, BN, AM', BN'` are perpendiculars to them respectively from the two given points :math:`A, B`. If :math:`MP : PN = M'P' : P'N'`, prove that the two tangents are parallel.
49. :math:`ABC` is a triangle inscribed in a circle; :math:`DE` is the diameter bisecting :math:`BC` at :math:`G`; from :math:`E` a perpendicular :math:`EK` is drawn to one of the sides, and the perpendicular from the vertex :math:`A` on :math:`DE` meets :math:`DE` in :math:`H`. Show that :math:`EK` touches the circle :math:`GHK`.
50. If the internal bisector of an angle of a triangle is equal to one of the including sides, show that the projection of the other side upon this bisector is equal to half the sum of the sides considered.
51. From two points, one on each of two opposite sides of a parallelogram, lines are drawn to the opposite vertices. Prove that the straight line through the points of intersection of these lines bisects the area of the parallelogram.
52. The point :math:`B` being the midpoint of the segment :math:`AC`, the circle :math:`(A, AB)` is drawn, and upon an arbitrary tangent to this circle the perpendicular :math:`CD` is dropped from the point :math:`C`. Show that angle :math:`ABD = 3\,BDC`.
53. Show that the line of centers of the two circles inscribed in the two right triangles into which a given right triangle is divided by the altitude to the hypotenuse is equal to the distance from the :term:`incenter <incircle>` of the given triangle to the vertex of its right angle.
54. :math:`ABC` is an equilateral triangle, :math:`D` a point on :math:`BC` such that :math:`BD` is one-third of :math:`BC`, and :math:`E` is a point on :math:`AB` equidistant from :math:`A` and :math:`D`. Show that :math:`CE = EB + BD`.
55. If a line :math:`AB` is bisected by :math:`C` and divided by :math:`D` unequally internally or externally, prove that :math:`AD^2 + DB^2 = 2(AC^2 + CD^2)`.
56. Let :math:`M` be the midpoint of chord :math:`AB` of a circle, center :math:`O`; on :math:`OM` as diameter draw another circle, and at any point :math:`T` of this circle draw a tangent to it meeting the outer circle in :math:`E`. Prove that :math:`AE^2 + BE^2 = 4\,ET^2`.
57. If :math:`M, N, P, Q` are the midpoints of the sides :math:`AB, BC, CD, DA` of a square :math:`ABCD`, prove that the intersections of the lines :math:`AN, BP, CQ, DM` determine a square of area one-fifth that of the given square :math:`ABCD`.
58. If :math:`M, N` are points on the sides :math:`AC, AB` of a triangle :math:`ABC` and the lines :math:`BM, CN` intersect on the altitude :math:`AD`, show that :math:`AD` is the bisector of the angle :math:`MDN`.
