Loci (59–67)
============

.. folio:: 0051
   :page: 32

59. :math:`A, B, C, D` are fixed points on a circle :math:`(O)`. The lines joining :math:`C, D` to a variable point :math:`P` meet :math:`(O)` again in :math:`Q, R`. Find the :term:`locus <geometric-locus>` of the second point of intersection :math:`S` of the two circles :math:`PQB, PRA`.
60. Find the :term:`locus <geometric-locus>` of a point at which two given circles subtend equal angles.
61. Given two points :math:`A, B`, collinear with the center :math:`O` of a given circle, and a variable diameter :math:`PQ` of this circle, find the :term:`locus <geometric-locus>` of the second point of intersection of the two circles :math:`APO, BQO`.
62. On the sides :math:`OA, OB` of a given angle :math:`O` two variable points :math:`A', B'` are marked so that the ratio :math:`AA' : BB'` is constant, and on the segment :math:`A'B'` the point :math:`I` is marked so that the ratio :math:`A'I : B'I` is constant. Prove that the :term:`locus <geometric-locus>` of the point :math:`I` is a straight line.

.. folio:: 0052
   :page: 33

63. A variable circle, passing through the vertex of a given angle, meets the sides of this angle in the points :math:`A, B`. Show that the :term:`locus <geometric-locus>` of the ends of the diameter parallel to the chord :math:`AB` consists of two straight lines.
64. :math:`AA', BB'` are two rectangular diameters of a given circle :math:`(O)`. A variable secant through :math:`B` meets :math:`(O)` in :math:`M` and :math:`AA'` in :math:`N`. Show that the point of intersection :math:`P` of the tangent to :math:`(O)` at :math:`M` with the perpendicular to :math:`AA'` at :math:`N` describes a straight line.
65. Through the center :math:`O` and the fixed point :math:`A` of a given circle :math:`(O)` a variable circle :math:`(C)` is drawn meeting :math:`(O)` again in :math:`D`. Find the :term:`locus <geometric-locus>` of the point of intersection :math:`M` of the tangents to the circle :math:`(C)` at the points :math:`O` and :math:`D`. Show that the line :math:`MC` is tangent to a fixed circle concentric with :math:`(O)`.
66. A variable circle touches the sides :math:`OB, OD` of a fixed angle in :math:`B` and :math:`D`; :math:`E` is the point of contact of this circle with the second tangent to it from a fixed point :math:`A` of the line :math:`OB`. Show that the line :math:`DE` passes through a fixed point.
67. A variable line :math:`PAB` through the fixed point :math:`P` meets the sides :math:`OA, OB` of a given angle :math:`O` in the points :math:`A, B`. On the lines :math:`OA, OB` the points :math:`A', B'` are constructed so that the ratios :math:`OA' : OA` and :math:`OB' : OB` are constant. Prove that the line :math:`A'B'` passes through a fixed point.
