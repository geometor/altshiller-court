§ 16. Remark
============

.. folio:: 0041
   :page: 22

The angles of the triangle :math:`AEF` are simply expressed in terms of the angles of :math:`ABC`, and one of the altitudes of :math:`AEF` belongs to :math:`ABC`. These relations afford a simple way of solving the following problems.

*Note.* Other problems involving the perimeter of a triangle will be discussed later (§§ 157–173).
