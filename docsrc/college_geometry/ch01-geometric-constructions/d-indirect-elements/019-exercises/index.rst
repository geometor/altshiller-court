§ 19. Exercises (1–3)
=====================

.. folio:: 0044
   :page: 25

Construct a triangle, given:

1. :math:`b - c, h_c, B - C`.
2. :math:`b - c, h_c, A`.
3. :math:`b, c, B - C`. *Hint.* Either :math:`BCD` or :math:`BCE` may be used as auxiliary triangle.
