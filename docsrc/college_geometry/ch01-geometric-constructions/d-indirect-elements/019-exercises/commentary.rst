Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

In ``FIG. 20`` (§ 19), because :math:`AE = AC = b` in isosceles :math:`\triangle ACE`, the base angles satisfy :math:`\angle AEC = \angle ACE = 90^\circ - \frac{1}{2}A`, whence :math:`\angle BCE = \angle ACE - C = 90^\circ - \frac{1}{2}A - C = \frac{1}{2}(B - C)` while :math:`\angle CBE = 180^\circ - B`, complementing the dual auxiliary triangle :math:`\triangle BCD` of § 18.
