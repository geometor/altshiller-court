Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Notice in Exercise 4 (:math:`h_c - h_b, A, b + c`) how the two *data* of §§ 21 and 22 interlock: from :math:`(h_c - h_b, A)` the subtractive *datum* yields :math:`b - c`, which combined with :math:`b + c` immediately determines the individual sides :math:`b` and :math:`c` together with the included angle :math:`A`! Supplementary Exercises 9–11 form a classic secant-through-circle-intersection suite solved by projecting the line of centers onto the variable secant (constructing a right triangle on the center-segment :math:`OO'` whose leg equals half the chord-sum, chord-difference, or chord-ratio projection).

Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Supplementary Exercise 15 reduces the perimeter of trapezoid :math:`BB'C'C` to an indirect sum along the sidelines :math:`AB, AC`, while Exercise 16 constructs a triangle whose sides are internally divided in given ratios by three fixed points (a classic affine-dilation / parallel-translation problem). In ``REVIEW EXERCISES — CONSTRUCTIONS``, items 1–13 synthesize all four pillars of Chapter I: translation of segments (item 7), capable-arc loci (items 1, 7), tangent-envelope circles for fixed chord lengths (items 2, 12), and positional circumcircle/incircle constraints (items 8–10).
