§ 19. Problem
=============

.. folio:: 0043
   :page: 24

.. enunciation::

   *Construct a* :g:`triangle <0044.g1:< A B C >>` *given the* :g:`base <0044.g1:/ B C />`\ *, the* :g:`difference of the other two sides <0044.g1:/ B E />`\ *, and the* :g:`altitude <0044.g1:/ C K />` *to one of these sides* :math:`(a, b - c, h_c)`.

Let :g:`ABC <0044.g1:< A B C >>` (Fig. 20) be the required triangle. Produce :g:`AB <0044.g1:[ B A ]>` and lay off :eq:`AE = AC <0044.g1:/ A E / = 0044.g1:/ A C />`, so that :g:`BE <0044.g1:/ B E />` :math:`= b - c`. In the triangle :g:`BCE <0044.g1:< B C E >>` we have :g:`BC <0044.g1:/ B C />` :math:`= a`, :g:`BE <0044.g1:/ B E />` :math:`= b - c`, and the :g:`altitude <0044.g1:/ C K />` to :g:`BE <0044.g1:[ B A ]>` equal to :math:`h_c`; hence this triangle may be constructed, and from this triangle we readily pass to the required triangle :g:`ABC <0044.g1:< A B C >>`.

.. folio:: 0044
   :page: 25

*Note.* The angles of :g:`BCE <0044.g1:< B C E >>` are determined in the same way as in the triangle :math:`BCD` of the preceding problem (§ 18), and these relations may be used to solve the following problems.

.. picture:: 0044.g1.svg
   :alt: Fig. 20 — Auxiliary triangle BCE formed by producing AB beyond B to E so that AE = b and BE = b - c with altitude h_c
   :caption: FIG. 20

Other problems involving the difference of two sides of a triangle will be considered later (§§ 157–173).
