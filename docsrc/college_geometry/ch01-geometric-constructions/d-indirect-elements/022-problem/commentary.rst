Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Section 22 is the exact subtractive counterpart to § 20: laying off :math:`FG = BE = h_b` along :math:`CF = h_c` isolates :math:`CG = h_c - h_b` inside right triangle :math:`\triangle CGH` on hypotenuse :math:`CH = b - c` opposite :math:`\angle CHG = A`, embodying the relation :math:`h_c - h_b = (b - c)\sin A`. Notice the printed alternate vertex order ``CHG`` in the penultimate line ('from the triangle CHG'), preserved diplomatically and resolved seamlessly via polygon permutation indexing.
