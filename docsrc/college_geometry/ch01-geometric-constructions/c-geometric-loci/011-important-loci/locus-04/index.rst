Locus 4
=======

.. folio:: 0031
   :page: 12

.. enunciation::

   *The locus of a point equidistant from two given points is the perpendicular bisector of the segment determined by the two points.*

For the sake of brevity the expression *perpendicular bisector* may be replaced conveniently by the term :term:`mediator <mediator>`.
