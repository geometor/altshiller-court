Section B — Stewart's Theorem
=============================

.. rubric:: Chapter VI: Transversals • Articles §§ 308–309 (Folios 0171–0172, pp. 152–153)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Triangle ABC with concurrent cevians AX, BY, CZ at Gergonne point Ge and Menelaus transversal UZY
   :caption: Ceva Concurrency at Gergonne Point X(7) and Harmonic Menelaus Transversal

Establishes :person:`Matthew Stewart's <stewart>` 1746 cevian theorem (**§§ 308–309**, :cite:`Stewart 1746 <stewart:1746>`), expressing the length of *any* segment from vertex :math:`A` to line :math:`BC` in terms of the side lengths and base segments.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§ 308 — Stewart's Theorem**: For any three collinear points :math:`B, C, D` on the base and arbitrary apex :math:`A`, signed segments obey :math:`AB^2 \cdot CD + AC^2 \cdot DB + AD^2 \cdot BC + BC \cdot CD \cdot DB = 0`.
* **§ 309 — Unified Cevian Corollaries**: Specializing :math:`D` to the midpoint or bisector trace recovering Apollonius' median formula and the angle-bisector length formula in a single stroke.

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

In ``geometor/wildberger``, Stewart's identity is a pure polynomial relation among quadrances requiring zero trigonometric functions or square roots.
