Section D — Ceva's Theorem (incl. Gergonne § 331, Nagel § 333, Desargues § 343)
===============================================================================

.. rubric:: Chapter VI: Transversals • Articles §§ 326–344 (Folios 0177–0184, pp. 158–165)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Triangle ABC with concurrent cevians AX, BY, CZ at Gergonne point Ge and Menelaus transversal UZY
   :caption: Ceva Concurrency at Gergonne Point X(7) and Harmonic Menelaus Transversal

Presents :person:`Giovanni Ceva's <ceva>` 1678 concurrency theorem (**§§ 326–344**, :cite:`Ceva 1678 <ceva:1678>`), pairing Menelaus collinearity with cevian concurrency and culminating in :person:`Desargues' <desargues>` two-triangle theorem.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 326–330 — Ceva's Theorem & Trigonometric Form**: Cevians :math:`AX, BY, CZ` concur if and only if :math:`\frac{BX}{XC} \cdot \frac{CY}{YA} \cdot \frac{AZ}{ZB} = +1` (and equivalent sine-ratio form), instantly proving concurrency of medians, altitudes, and angle bisectors.
* **§§ 331–336 — Gergonne Point, Nagel Point & Isotomic Conjugates**: Proves concurrency of incircle-contact cevians at the :term:`Gergonne point <gergonne-point>` :math:`Ge = X(7)` (**§ 331**, :person:`Gergonne <gergonne>`), excircle-contact cevians at the :term:`Nagel point <nagel-point>` :math:`Na = X(8)` (**§ 333**, :person:`Nagel <nagel>`), and general :term:`isotomic conjugation <isotomic-conjugates>`.
* **§§ 337–344 — Harmonic Cevian–Transversal Duality & Desargues' Theorem**: Shows that if :math:`AX, BY, CZ` concur, line :math:`YZ` meets :math:`BC` at the harmonic conjugate of :math:`X`, and proves :person:`Desargues' <desargues>` perspective-triangles theorem (**§ 343**).

-----------------------------------
