Neuberg, Joseph Jean Baptiste
=============================

*1840–1926*

* **Role**: Luxembourgish-Belgian Geometer, University of Liège
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 606, § 616, § 654, § 677, § 679, § 680, § 690, § 693
* **Principal Works**: *Sur le point de Steiner (1886)*; *Mémoire sur le tétraèdre (1884)*; *Sur les projections d'une droite sur les côtés d'un triangle (orthopole)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0031 (p. 12) <ch01-locus-07>` · :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Co-founder (with Mansion) of Mathesis and co-architect (with Lemoine and Brocard) of modern triangle geometry; discovered the isodynamic points, Neuberg circles, and the orthopole (§ 690).
