Plücker, Julius
===============

*1801–1868*

* **Role**: German Geometer & Physicist
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 421, § 440, § 518
* **Principal Works**: *Analytisch-geometrische Entwicklungen (1828–1831)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Introduced trilinear coordinates, line geometry, and algebraic formulations of the radical axis and coaxal circles.
