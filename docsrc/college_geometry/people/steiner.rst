Steiner, Jakob
==============

*1796–1863*

* **Role**: Swiss Geometer, University of Berlin
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 207, § 215, § 282, § 299, § 412, § 440, § 470, § 506, § 518, § 626, § 679
* **Principal Works**: *Einige geometrische Betrachtungen (Crelle's Journal, Vol. 1, 1826)*; *Systematische Entwicklung der Abhängigkeit geometrischer Gestalten von einander (1832)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Regarded as the purest synthetic geometer since Apollonius. Introduced the 'power of a point' with respect to a circle (1826, Ch. VIII.E, § 412), proved fundamental theorems on the Simson line envelope (Steiner deltoid, § 299), complete quadrilaterals, inversion, the Problem of Apollonius, and the Steiner point R = X(99) on the circumcircle (§ 679).
