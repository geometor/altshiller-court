Viète, François
===============

*1540–1603*

* **Role**: French Mathematician
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 506, § 516
* **Principal Works**: *Apollonius Gallus (1600)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0328 (p. 309) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Reconstructed Apollonius's lost Tangencies in his Apollonius Gallus (1600), solving the ten cases of the Problem of Apollonius sequentially via centers of similitude and inversion precursors (§§ 506–516).
