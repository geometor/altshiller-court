Castillon, Johann (Salvemini, Giovanni Francesco)
=================================================

*1709–1791*

* **Role**: Italian-Swiss Geometer, Berlin Academy
* **Articles in College Geometry**: Frontmatter / Apparatus
* **Principal Works**: *Sur un problème de géométrie plane (Nouveaux Mémoires de l'Académie de Berlin, 1776)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0027 (p. 8) <ch01-art-009>`

Solved in 1776 the celebrated Cramer-Castillon Problem of inscribing in a given circle a triangle whose sides pass through three given points—a projective-constructive counterpart to Chapter I's incidence problems (§ 9, § 14).
