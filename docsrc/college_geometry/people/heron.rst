Heron of Alexandria
===================

*fl. c. 60 AD*

* **Role**: Graeco-Egyptian Mathematician & Engineer
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 12, § 132
* **Principal Works**: *Metrica*; *Catoptrica*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Formulated Heron's area formula for a triangle in terms of its semiperimeter and solved the shortest-path reflection problem on a line (§ 12).
