Miquel, Auguste
===============

*1816–1851*

* **Role**: French Mathematician
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 282, § 650
* **Principal Works**: *Théorèmes de géométrie (Journal de Liouville, 1838)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Discovered Miquel's pivot theorem (1838) on the concurrency of circles through triangle vertices and side points, as well as the Miquel point of a complete quadrilateral.
