Gauss, Carl Friedrich
=====================

*1777–1855*

* **Role**: German Mathematician & Astronomer
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 175, § 353
* **Principal Works**: *Disquisitiones Arithmeticae (1801)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0326 (p. 307) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Proved the collinearity of the midpoints of the three diagonals of a complete quadrilateral (the Gauss-Newton line) and studied triangle altitudes and inversion precursors.
