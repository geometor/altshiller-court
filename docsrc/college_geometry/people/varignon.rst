Varignon, Pierre
================

*1654–1722*

* **Role**: French Mathematician & Mechanician
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 240
* **Principal Works**: *Élémens de mathématique (1731)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0328 (p. 309) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Proved Varignon's Theorem (published posthumously 1731) that the midpoints of the sides of any quadrilateral form a parallelogram (§ 240).
