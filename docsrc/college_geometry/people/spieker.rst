Spieker, Theodor
================

*1823–1913*

* **Role**: German Geometer (Potsdam)
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 333
* **Principal Works**: *Lehrbuch der ebenen Geometrie (1862)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Author of Lehrbuch der ebenen Geometrie (1862); investigated the incircle of the medial (complementary) triangle—the Spieker circle, centered at the Spieker point X(10), center of mass of the triangle's perimeter.
