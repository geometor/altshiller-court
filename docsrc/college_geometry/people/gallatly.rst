Gallatly, William
=================

*c. 1850 – c. 1928*

* **Role**: English Geometer
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 639, § 690, § 697
* **Principal Works**: *The Modern Geometry of the Triangle (2nd ed., 1913)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0326 (p. 307) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Author of The Modern Geometry of the Triangle (`Gy.`, 2nd ed. 1913), frequently cited by Altshiller-Court on Lemoine, Brocard, Tucker, and orthopole theorems.
