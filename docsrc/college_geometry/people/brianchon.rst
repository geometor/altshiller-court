Brianchon, Charles Julien
=========================

*1783–1864*

* **Role**: French Projective Geometer
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 207, § 224, § 381, § 449
* **Principal Works**: *Recherches sur la détermination d'une hyperbole équilatère (with Poncelet, 1820–1821)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0326 (p. 307) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Student at the École Polytechnique under Monge. With Poncelet (1821), published the first complete proof of the Nine-Point Circle theorem and discovered Brianchon's hexagram theorem.
