Poncelet, Jean-Victor
=====================

*1788–1867*

* **Role**: French Engineer & Architect of Projective Geometry
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 31, § 207, § 358, § 374, § 394, § 440, § 447, § 460, § 463, § 518
* **Principal Works**: *Traité des propriétés projectives des figures (1822)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Wrote Traité des propriétés projectives des figures (1822), initially drafted as a prisoner of war at Saratov (1813–1814). Established continuity, ideal chords, limiting points of coaxal circles (Poncelet's coaxal theorems, Ch. VIII.G), homothecy, and (with Brianchon) the Nine-Point Circle.
