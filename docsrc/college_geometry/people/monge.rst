Monge, Gaspard
==============

*1746–1818*

* **Role**: French Geometer & Founder of the École Polytechnique
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 405, § 406, § 421
* **Principal Works**: *Géométrie descriptive (1799)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Creator of descriptive geometry and teacher of Carnot, Poncelet, Brianchon, and Dupin. Proved Monge's Theorem that the external centers of similitude of three circles taken in pairs are collinear (§ 405).
