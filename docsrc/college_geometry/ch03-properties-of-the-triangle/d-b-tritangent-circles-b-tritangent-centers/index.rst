Section D.b — Tritangent Circles: Tritangent Centers
====================================================

.. rubric:: Chapter III: Properties of the Triangle • Articles §§ 117–130 (Folios 0092–0097, pp. 73–78)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Triangle ABC with circumcircle, angle bisectors concurring at incenter I, incircle, and contact radii IX, IY, IZ
   :caption: Incircle (I, r), Angle Bisectors, Contact Points X, Y, Z, and Chapple–Euler Relation

Establishes the concurrency of the internal and external angle bisectors at the :term:`incenter <incircle>` :math:`I = X(1)` and the three excenters :math:`I_a, I_b, I_c` (**§§ 117–130**), revealing their orthocentric duality with :math:`\triangle ABC`.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 117–121 — Existence of the Four Tritangent Centers**: Three internal bisectors concur at :math:`I`, while each internal bisector concurs with two external bisectors at :math:`I_a, I_b, I_c`, forming an :term:`orthocentric group <orthocentric-quadrilateral>` for which :math:`\triangle ABC` is the :term:`orthic triangle <orthic-triangle>`!
* **§§ 122–126 — Trident (Incenter–Excenter) Lemma**: If the bisector :math:`AII_a` meets the circumcircle at :math:`M`, then :math:`M` is the midpoint of :math:`II_a` and :math:`MB = MC = MI = MI_a`.
* **§§ 127–130 — Circumcircle of the Excentral Triangle**: Shows that the circumcircle of :math:`\triangle I_a I_b I_c` has radius :math:`2R` and is centered at the reflection of :math:`I` across the circumcenter :math:`O` (:math:`X(40)`, the Bevan point).

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

Demonstrates in ``geometor/etc`` how the map :math:`\triangle I_a I_b I_c \mapsto \triangle ABC` interchanges excentral/orthic invariants, sending orthocenter to incenter :math:`X(1)` and nine-point circle to circumcircle.
