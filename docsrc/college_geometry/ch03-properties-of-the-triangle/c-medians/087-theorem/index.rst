§ 87. Theorem
=============

.. folio:: 0084
   :page: 65

.. enunciation::

   *\(a) The line joining the midpoints of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side and is equal to one-half its length.*

   *\(b) The line drawn parallel to a side of a triangle through the midpoint of another side passes through the midpoint of the third side.*

----
