§ 56. Remark II
===============

.. folio:: 0073
   :page: 54

.. picture:: 0072.g1.svg
   :alt: Fig. 39 — Internal and external division of segment AB in ratio p : q via parallel lines AF and GBH
   :caption: FIG. 39

If :math:`p > q`, we have :math:`AC > CB` and :math:`AD > DB`, so that the midpoint :g:`M <0072.g1:M>` of :g:`AB <0072.g1:/ A B />` lies outside the segment :g:`CD <0072.g1:/ C D />`. The same relative position of the points :g:`M <0072.g1:M>`, :g:`C <0072.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0072.g1:D>` prevails, if :math:`p < q`.

If :math:`p = q`, the point :g:`C <0072.g1:C>` coincides with :g:`M <0072.g1:M>`, and there is no external point of division, since :g:`FH <0072.g1:[ F H ]>` is then parallel to :g:`AB <0072.g1:[ A B ]>`.
