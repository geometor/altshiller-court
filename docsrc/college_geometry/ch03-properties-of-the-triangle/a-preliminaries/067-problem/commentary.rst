Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Problem § 67 is Carlyle's / Steiner's classic circle-secant ruler-and-compass solver for monic quadratic equations :math:`x^2 \mp ax \pm t^2 = 0` via the :term:`power of a point <power-of-a-point>` (:math:`BM \cdot BM' = BC \cdot BD = t^2`). In ``FIG. 40a`` (sum :math:`a`), real roots exist iff the circle reaches :math:`AB`, i.e. :math:`a \ge 2t` (:math:`a^2 - 4t^2 \ge 0`); in ``FIG. 40b`` (difference :math:`a`), :math:`C` and :math:`D` lie on opposite sides of :math:`AB` so the circle always intersects :math:`AB` in real roots! Altshiller-Court invokes § 67 directly in § 83 to solve :math:`x(x + t) = 2Rm` on the way to the :term:`Problem of Pappus <problem-of-pappus>` (§ 84).
