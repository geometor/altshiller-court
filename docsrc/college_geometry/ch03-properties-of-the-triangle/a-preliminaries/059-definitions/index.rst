§ 59. Definitions
=================

.. folio:: 0073
   :page: 54

.. picture:: 0072.g1.svg
   :alt: Fig. 39 — Internal and external division of segment AB in ratio p : q via parallel lines AF and GBH
   :caption: FIG. 39

Instead of saying that the points :g:`C <0072.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0072.g1:D>` (§ 57) divide the segment :g:`AB <0072.g1:/ A B />` internally and externally in the same ratio, we shall sometimes say that the points :g:`C <0072.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0072.g1:D>` *divide the segment* :math:`AB` :term:`*harmonically* <harmonic-division>`, or that the points :g:`C <0072.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0072.g1:D>` *separate the points* :math:`A, B` *harmonically*, or that the points :g:`C <0072.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0072.g1:D>` are :term:`*harmonic conjugates* <harmonic-division>` with respect to the points :g:`A <0072.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0072.g1:B>`.

The preceding proposition (§ 57) states that the relation between the two pairs of points is mutual, so that the points :g:`A <0072.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0072.g1:B>`, in turn, divide the segment :g:`CD <0072.g1:/ C D />` harmonically, and the points :g:`A <0072.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0072.g1:B>` separate the points :g:`C <0072.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0072.g1:D>` harmonically. We may thus refer to the two pairs of points :math:`A, B` and :math:`C, D` as *two pairs of harmonic points*, and to the two segments :g:`AB <0072.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`CD <0072.g1:/ C D />` as *two harmonic segments*.
