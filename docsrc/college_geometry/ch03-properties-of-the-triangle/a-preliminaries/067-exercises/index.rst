§ 67. Exercises (1–3)
=====================

.. folio:: 0076
   :page: 57

1. Place two segments, of given lengths, on the same line so that they shall be :term:`harmonic <harmonic-division>`. *Hint.* Use § 66.
2. Show that the pairs of bisectors of the angles of a triangle determine on the respectively opposite sides three segments such that the reciprocal of one is equal to the sum of the reciprocals of the other two. *Hint.* Use § 58.
3. Show that the internal (or external) bisector of an angle of a triangle is divided harmonically by the feet of the perpendiculars dropped upon it from the two other vertices of the triangle.

----
