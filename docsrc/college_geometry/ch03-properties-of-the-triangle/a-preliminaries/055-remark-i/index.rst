§ 55. Remark I
==============

.. folio:: 0072
   :page: 53

.. picture:: 0072.g1.svg
   :alt: Fig. 39 — Internal and external division of segment AB in ratio p : q via parallel lines AF and GBH
   :caption: FIG. 39

To avoid ambiguity the statement of the problem should indicate whether the segments of :g:`AB <0072.g1:/ A B />` proportional to :math:`p` and :math:`q` shall be adjacent to :g:`A <0072.g1:A>` and :g:`B <0072.g1:B>`, respectively, as is the case of the figure, or to :g:`B <0072.g1:B>` and :g:`A <0072.g1:A>`. Otherwise there is another pair of points, :math:`C', D'`, the symmetric of :g:`C <0072.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0072.g1:D>` with respect to the midpoint :g:`M <0072.g1:M>` of :g:`AB <0072.g1:/ A B />`, which solves the problem. However, in actual applications the nature of the problem usually distinguishes between the points :g:`A <0072.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0072.g1:B>` and thus leads to only one pair of points of division.
