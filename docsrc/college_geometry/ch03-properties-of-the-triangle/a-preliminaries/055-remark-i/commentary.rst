Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Problem § 54 supplies the canonical ruler-and-compass construction promised in § 2 for dividing a segment :math:`AB` both internally at :math:`C` and externally at :math:`D` in the same ratio :math:`p : q` by laying off parallel directed intercepts :math:`AF = p` and :math:`BG = BH = q`. Because :math:`(A, B; C, D)` is a harmonic range (§ 59), ``FIG. 39`` also marks the midpoint :math:`M` of :math:`AB` (tick mark) and the midpoint :math:`O` of :math:`CD` (dot), preparing the metric harmonic theorems of §§ 62–66.

Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Theorem § 57 proves the fundamental reciprocity of harmonic division: if :math:`(A, B; C, D) = -1` with ratio :math:`p : q`, then :math:`(D, C; B, A) = -1` with dual ratio :math:`(p + q) : (p - q)`. When :math:`p = q`, :math:`C` becomes the midpoint :math:`M` of :math:`AB` and :math:`D` recedes to the ideal point at infinity on :math:`AB`, foreshadowing the projective treatment of Chapter VII (§ 345).
