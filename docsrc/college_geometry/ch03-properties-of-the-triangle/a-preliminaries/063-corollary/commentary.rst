Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Three foundational metric characterizations of harmonic ranges appear on this page:

1. **Newton's Relation (§ 63)**: :math:`OA \cdot OB = OC^2 = OD^2`, showing that :math:`A` and :math:`B` are :term:`inverse points <inverse-points>` with respect to the circle on diameter :math:`CD`—and since :math:`OA : OB = p^2 : q^2` (§ 62), this circle is precisely the :term:`Apollonian circle <apollonian-circle>` of locus ratio :math:`p : q`.
2. **Orthogonality Invariant (§ 66)**: Writing :math:`AB = 2r_1` and :math:`CD = 2r_2`, the identity :math:`AB^2 + CD^2 = 4\,MO^2` becomes :math:`r_1^2 + r_2^2 = MO^2`, proving that the circles on diameters :math:`AB` and :math:`CD` are :term:`orthogonal circles <orthogonal-circles>` (§ 368).
