§ 62. Theorem
=============

.. folio:: 0074
   :page: 55

.. enunciation::

   *If the points* :g:`C <0072.g1:C>`\ *,* :g:`D <0072.g1:D>` *divide the segment* :g:`AB <0072.g1:/ A B />` *harmonically in the ratio* :math:`p : q`\ *, the midpoint* :g:`O <0072.g1:O>` *of the segment* :g:`CD <0072.g1:/ C D />` *divides the segment* :g:`AB <0072.g1:/ A B />` *externally in the ratio* :math:`p^2 : q^2`\ *.*

.. picture:: 0072.g1.svg
   :alt: Fig. 39 — Internal and external division of segment AB in ratio p : q via parallel lines AF and GBH
   :caption: FIG. 39

The point :g:`O <0072.g1:O>` lies outside the segment :g:`AB <0072.g1:/ A B />` (§ 56). Now:

.. math::

   AO = \tfrac{1}{2}\,CD + AC, \quad BO = \tfrac{1}{2}\,CD - BC.

Substituting for :g:`CD <0072.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`AC <0072.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`BC <0072.g1:/ C B />` their values (§§ 57, 58), we obtain:

.. math::

   AO = ap^2 : (p^2 - q^2), \quad BO = aq^2 : (p^2 - q^2);

hence the announced relation.
