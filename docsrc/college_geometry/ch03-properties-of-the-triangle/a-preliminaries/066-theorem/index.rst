§ 66. Theorem
=============

.. folio:: 0074
   :page: 55

.. enunciation::

   *The sum of the squares of two harmonic segments is equal to four times the square of the distance between the midpoints of these segments.*

We have (§ 58):

.. math::

   AB^2 + CD^2 = a^2 + 4a^2p^2q^2 : (p^2 - q^2)^2 = a^2(p^2 + q^2)^2 : (p^2 - q^2)^2;

hence (§ 64):

.. math::

   AB^2 + CD^2 = 4\,MO^2.
