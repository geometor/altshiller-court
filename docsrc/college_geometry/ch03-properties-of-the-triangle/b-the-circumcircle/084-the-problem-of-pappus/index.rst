§ 84. The Problem of Pappus
===========================

.. folio:: 0082
   :page: 63

.. enunciation::

   *Through a given point on the bisector of a given angle to draw a secant on which the given angle shall intercept a segment of given length.*

.. picture:: 0082.g1.svg
   :alt: Fig. 46 — Secant EUA through arc midpoint E meeting chord BC in U with intercepted segment AU = t
   :caption: FIG. 46

.. folio:: 0083
   :page: 64

Let :g:`B <0082.g1:B>`, :g:`C <0082.g1:C>` (Fig. 46) be the traces of the required secant :math:`UBC = a` on the sides of the given angle :g:`BAC <0082.g1:< B A C )>`; the circumradius of the triangle :g:`ABC <0082.g1:< A B C >>` is determined (§ 69), and in the :g:`circumcircle <0082.g1:( A B C )>` the bisector :g:`AU <0082.g1:/ A U />` of the angle :g:`A <0082.g1:A>` passes through the midpoint :g:`E <0082.g1:E>` of the arc :math:`BEC` subtended by :math:`BC = a`. We are thus led to the following construction.

On an arbitrary line lay off :math:`B'C'` equal to the given length :math:`a`, and on :math:`B'C'` as chord construct the :term:`locus <subtended-angle-locus>` of the points at which :math:`B'C'` subtends an angle equal to the given angle :g:`A <0082.g1:A>` (§ 11, locus 7). Through the midpoint :math:`E'` of the other arc of this circle draw a secant :math:`E'U'A'` meeting :math:`B'C'` in :math:`U'` and the circle again in :math:`A'`, so that :math:`U'A'` shall be equal to the given segment :g:`AU <0082.g1:/ A U />` of the bisector of the given angle (§ 83).

The line through :g:`U <0082.g1:U>`, on the bisector of the given angle, making with :g:`AU <0082.g1:/ A U />` an angle equal to the angle :math:`B'U'A'`, solves the proposed problem.

The problem has two solutions.
