Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Both § 76 :math:`(h_a, m_a, t_a)` and § 78 :math:`(a, m_a, B - C)` construct the :term:`circumcenter <circumcenter>` :math:`O` directly before finding :math:`B` and :math:`C`! In § 76, right triangles :math:`ADA'` and :math:`ADU` fix :math:`A, D, U, A'`, and :math:`O` is the intersection of the perpendicular to :math:`BC` at :math:`A'` with the isogonal ray of :math:`AD` across :math:`AU`. In § 78, intersecting chords :math:`A'A \cdot A'J = A'B \cdot A'C = \tfrac{1}{4}a^2` determine the chord extension :math:`A'J = a^2 / (4m_a)`, so :math:`O` is determined as the intersection of the :term:`mediator <mediator>` of :math:`AJ` with the capable arc :math:`\angle AOA' = 180^\circ - (B - C)`.
