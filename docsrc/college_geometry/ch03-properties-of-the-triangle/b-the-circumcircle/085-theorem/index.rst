§ 85. Theorem
=============

.. folio:: 0083
   :page: 64

.. enunciation::

   *The product of two sides of a* :g:`*triangle* <0079.g1:< A B C >>` *is equal to the* :g:`*altitude* <0079.g1:/ A D />` *to the third side multiplied by the* :g:`*circumdiameter* <0079.g1:/ A K />`\ *.*

.. picture:: 0079.g1.svg
   :alt: Fig. 44 — Circumdiameter AK through circumcenter O, altitude AD, bisector AU, and median AA' of triangle ABC
   :caption: FIG. 44

The angle :eq:`ABD = AKC <0079.g1:< A B D ) = 0079.g1:< A K C )>` (Fig. 44); hence, from the similar right triangles:

.. math::

   AB : AK = AD : AC, \quad \text{or} \quad AB \cdot AC = AK \cdot AD,

i.e.,

.. math::

   bc = 2R \cdot h_a.
