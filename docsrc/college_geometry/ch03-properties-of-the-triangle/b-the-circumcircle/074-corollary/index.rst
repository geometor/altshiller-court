§ 74. Corollary
===============

.. folio:: 0079
   :page: 60

.. enunciation::

   *In the right triangle* :g:`ADU <0079.g1:< A D U >>` *we have* :math:`AD = h_a`\ *,* :math:`AU = t_a`\ *, and angle* :math:`DAU = \tfrac{1}{2}(B - C)`\ *, i.e., we have the datum:*

   .. math::

      h_a,\quad t_a,\quad B - C.
