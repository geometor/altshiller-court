Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Problem § 72 combines Chapter I's auxiliary-triangle extension (:math:`AD = AC` along :math:`BA` produced, § 15) with Euclid III.36 (:math:`DE^2 = DA \cdot DB = b(b + c)`), locating :math:`D` at the intersection of a :term:`capable arc <subtended-angle-locus>` (:math:`\angle BDC = \tfrac{1}{2}A`) and a circle concentric with :math:`(O)` of radius :math:`DO = \sqrt{R^2 + b(b + c)}`. At the bottom of the page, Theorem § 73 introduces one of the most celebrated properties of the :term:`circumcenter <circumcenter>`: the circumradius :math:`AO` and altitude :math:`AD` are :term:`isogonal conjugates <isogonal-conjugates>` with respect to :math:`\angle A` (§ 625)!
