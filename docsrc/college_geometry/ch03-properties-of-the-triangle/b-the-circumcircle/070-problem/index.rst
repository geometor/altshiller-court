§ 70. Problem
=============

.. folio:: 0076
   :page: 57

.. enunciation::

   *Construct a* :g:`*triangle* <0076.g1:< A B C >>` *given the* :g:`*base* <0076.g1:/ B C />`\ *, the* :g:`*vertical angle* <0076.g1:< B A C )>`\ *, and the ratio of the distances of the* :g:`*midpoint* <0076.g1:Aprime>` *of the base from the traces, on the base, of the* :g:`*altitude* <0076.g1:/ A D />` *and of the* :g:`*bisector* <0076.g1:/ A U />` *of the vertical angle.*

.. picture:: 0076.g1.svg
   :alt: Fig. 41 — Triangle ABC inscribed in its circumcircle with altitude AD, bisector AU, midpoint A', and arc midpoint E
   :caption: FIG. 41

Let :g:`ABC <0076.g1:< A B C >>` (Fig. 41) be the required triangle. Let :g:`A' <0076.g1:Aprime>` be the midpoint of :g:`BC <0076.g1:/ B C />`, and :g:`D <0076.g1:D>`, :g:`U <0076.g1:U>` the traces on :g:`BC <0076.g1:/ B C />` of the altitude :g:`AD <0076.g1:/ A D />` and the bisector :g:`AU <0076.g1:/ A U />` of the angle :g:`A <0076.g1:< B A C )>`. The bisector :g:`AU <0076.g1:[ A U ]>`, produced, and the :term:`mediator <mediator>` of :g:`BC <0076.g1:/ B C />` both bisect the arc :math:`BC`, say, in the point :g:`E <0076.g1:E>`.

.. folio:: 0077
   :page: 58

.. assertion::

   From the similar right triangles :g:`ADU <0076.g1:< A D U >>`, :g:`EA'U <0076.g1:< E A' U >>` we have:

   .. math::

      A'U : UD = A'E : AD.

Now the base :g:`BC <0076.g1:/ B C />` and the angle :g:`A <0076.g1:< B A C )>` being given, the :term:`circumcircle <circumcircle>` :g:`ABC <0076.g1:( A B C )>` is determined; hence the segment :g:`A'E <0076.g1:/ A' E />` is known; also the first ratio in the above proportion is known; hence the altitude :g:`AD <0076.g1:/ A D />` may be constructed. The solution of the problem is now readily completed.
