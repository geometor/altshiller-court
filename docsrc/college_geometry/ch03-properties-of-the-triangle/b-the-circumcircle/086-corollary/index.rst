§ 86. Corollary
===============

.. folio:: 0083
   :page: 64

.. enunciation::

   *The area of a triangle is equal to the product of its three sides divided by the double circumdiameter of the triangle.*

.. folio:: 0084
   :page: 65

Indeed, multiplying both sides of the above equality (§ 85) by :math:`a` and observing that :math:`ah_a = 2S`, where :math:`S` is the area of the triangle, we have:

.. math::

   abc = 4RS.

----
