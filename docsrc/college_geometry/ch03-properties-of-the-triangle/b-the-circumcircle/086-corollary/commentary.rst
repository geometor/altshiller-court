Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- **Construction of the Problem of Pappus (§ 84)**: Since the position of the required triangle is not yet fixed on the sides of :math:`\angle BAC`, :person:`Altshiller-Court <altshiller-court>` constructs a congruent auxiliary configuration on an arbitrary segment :math:`B'C' = a` via the capable-arc locus (**§ 11, Locus 7**) and circular secant insertion (**§ 83**), then transfers the angle :math:`\angle B'U'A'` back to the given bisector at :math:`U`. Reflection across the bisector yields the two symmetric solutions.
- **Equal Internal and External Bisectors (Exercises 8–10)**: In :math:`\triangle ABC`, :math:`t_a = t_a'` holds if and only if right triangle :math:`UAU'` is isosceles, i.e. :math:`|B - C| = 90^\circ`, which is equivalent to :math:`AO \parallel BC` (since :math:`\angle BAO = 90^\circ - C`).
- **Circumdiameter Product Formula (§ 85)**: Similar right triangles :math:`\triangle ABD \sim \triangle AKC` (:cite:`euclid:elements` VI.C) immediately yield :math:`bc = 2Rh_a`, one of the cornerstones of classical and modern triangle metric geometry.

Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- **Circumradius–Area Formula (§ 86)**: Multiplying :math:`bc = 2Rh_a` (§ 85) by :math:`a` yields :math:`abc = 4RS`, linking the :term:`circumcircle <circumcircle>` radius :math:`R` directly to the side product and area :math:`S` (compare :person:`Heron <heron>`'s formula in § 132). Exercise 4 states a classic :person:`Pappus <pappus>`-type locus equality on the circumcircle relative to :math:`\triangle ABC` and its tangential triangle.
- **Medians and the Centroid (§§ 87–88)**: Using the midline theorem (:cite:`euclid:elements` VI.2, :math:`B'C' \parallel BC` and :math:`B'C' = \tfrac{1}{2}BC`), :person:`Altshiller-Court <altshiller-court>` proves :math:`\triangle GBC \sim \triangle GB'C'` with ratio :math:`2 : 1`, establishing the concurrency of the three medians at the :term:`centroid <centroid>` :math:`G = X(2)` (:cite:`kimberling:etc`; :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>`, *On the Equilibrium of Planes* I.14). Notice that ``FIG. 47`` already includes auxiliary midpoints :math:`M, N` of :math:`BG, CG` and point :math:`K` on :math:`AA'` produced (used in the alternative parallelogram proofs and exercises on p. 66).
