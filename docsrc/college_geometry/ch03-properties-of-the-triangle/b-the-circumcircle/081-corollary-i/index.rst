§ 81. Corollary I
=================

.. folio:: 0081
   :page: 62

.. enunciation::

   *If the* :g:`*tangent* <0081.g1:/ T A />` *at* :g:`A <0081.g1:A>` *to the* :g:`*circumcircle* <0081.g1:( A B C )>` *meets* :g:`BC <0081.g1:[ B C ]>` *in* :g:`T <0081.g1:T>`\ *, we have* :eq:`TA = TU <0081.g1:/ T A / = 0081.g1:/ T U />` :math:`=` :g:`TU' <0081.g1:/ T U' />`\ *.*

.. picture:: 0081.g1.svg
   :alt: Fig. 45 — Internal and external bisectors AU, AU', circumdiameter LL' perpendicular to BC, and tangent AT
   :caption: FIG. 45

Indeed, we have (Fig. 45):

.. math::

   \angle TAU = TAB + BAU, \quad \angle TUA = UCA + UAC.

Now angle :math:`TAB = UCA`, and angle :math:`BAU = UAC`; hence the triangle :g:`TAU <0081.g1:< T A U >>` is isosceles, and :eq:`TA = TU <0081.g1:/ T A / = 0081.g1:/ T U />`.

The circle :math:`(T, TA)` will pass through :g:`U <0081.g1:U>`, for :eq:`TA = TU <0081.g1:/ T A / = 0081.g1:/ T U />`, and since :g:`UAU' <0081.g1:< U A U' )>` is a right angle, the circle will also pass through :g:`U' <0081.g1:Uprime>`; hence :eq:`TA = TU' <0081.g1:/ T A / = 0081.g1:/ T U' />`.
