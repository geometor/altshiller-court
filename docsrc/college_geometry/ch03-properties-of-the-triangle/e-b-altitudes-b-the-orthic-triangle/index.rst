Section E.b — Altitudes: The Orthic Triangle
============================================

.. rubric:: Chapter III: Properties of the Triangle • Articles §§ 183–200 (Folios 0116–0119, pp. 97–100)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Acute triangle ABC with altitudes AD, BE, CF meeting at H, highlighted orthic triangle DEF, and circumscribed nine-point circle

Investigates the :term:`orthic (pedal) triangle <orthic-triangle>` :math:`\triangle DEF` formed by the feet of the three altitudes (**§§ 183–200**), uncovering its antiparallel sides, optical reflection law, and :person:`Fagnano's <fagnano>` minimum-perimeter theorem.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 183–189 — Antiparallel Sides & Altitudes as Bisectors**: Cyclic quadrilaterals :math:`BCEF, CAFD, ABDE` render :math:`EF, FD, DE` antiparallel to :math:`BC, CA, AB`; the altitudes of an acute :math:`\triangle ABC` bisect the interior angles of :math:`\triangle DEF`, making :math:`H` the incenter of :math:`\triangle DEF`!
* **§§ 190–193 — Circumradius perpendicularity**: Each circumradius :math:`OA, OB, OC` is perpendicular to the corresponding orthic side :math:`EF, FD, DE` (:person:`Nagel's <nagel>` theorem).
* **§§ 194–200 — Fagnano's Problem (1775)**: Among all triangles inscribed in an acute triangle :math:`ABC`, the :term:`orthic triangle <orthic-triangle>` :math:`\triangle DEF` uniquely minimizes the perimeter, equal to :math:`2S / R` (:person:`Fagnano <fagnano>`).

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

Illustrated symbolically in the ``orthic_fagnano`` hero model via exact Rational Trigonometry spread equality :math:`s(FD, DA) = s(DA, DE)`.
