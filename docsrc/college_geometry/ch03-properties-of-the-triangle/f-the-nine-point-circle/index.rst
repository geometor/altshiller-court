Section F — The Nine-Point Circle (incl. Feuerbach's Theorem § 215)
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Chapter III: Properties of the Triangle • Articles §§ 206–219 (Folios 0122–0127, pp. 103–108)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Exact symbolic diagram of triangle ABC with circumcircle, Euler line OGNH, nine-point circle, incircle, and Feuerbach tangency point Fe
   :caption: Euler Line O–G–N–H, Nine-Point Circle (N), Incircle (I), and Feuerbach Point X(11)

Presents two jewels of nineteenth-century synthetic geometry (**§§ 206–219**): the :term:`nine-point circle <nine-point-circle>` (:person:`Euler <euler>`, :person:`Brianchon <brianchon>`, :person:`Poncelet <poncelet>`) and :term:`Feuerbach's theorem <feuerbach-theorem>` (**§ 215**, :cite:`Feuerbach 1822 <feuerbach:1822>`).

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 206–210 — The Nine-Point Circle**: The three side midpoints :math:`A', B', C'`, the three altitude feet :math:`D, E, F`, and the three Euler points (midpoints of :math:`HA, HB, HC`) lie on a single circle of radius :math:`R/2` centered at :math:`N = X(5)`, the midpoint of :math:`OH`.
* **§§ 211–214 — Homothecy from H and G**: The nine-point circle is homothetic to the circumcircle from :math:`H` with ratio :math:`+\tfrac{1}{2}` (bisecting every segment from :math:`H` to :math:`(O)`) and from :math:`G` with ratio :math:`-\tfrac{1}{2}`.
* **§§ 215–219 — Feuerbach's Theorem (1822)**: The nine-point circle of a triangle is internally tangent to the :term:`incircle <incircle>` at the Feuerbach point :math:`Fe = X(11)` (:math:`NI = \tfrac{1}{2}R - r`) and externally tangent to each of the three excircles (:math:`NI_a = \tfrac{1}{2}R + r_a`).
