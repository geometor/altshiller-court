Content of type 'chapter'

Frontmatter

Nineteen archival frontmatter folios (0001–0019, pp. [cover]–[xx]) framing Nathan Altshiller-Court’s 1952 Second Edition: the Dover cover, title and copyright pages, dedication, 1952 Preface and Acknowledgments, analytical Table of Contents, To the Instructor, and To the Student.

Proposition · Commentary

Chapter I: Geometric Constructions

Foundational treatise on synthetic ruler-and-compass problem solving (§§ 1–22, Folios 0020–0052, pp. 1–33): notation and basic constructions, Pappus’s four-part analytic method, twelve fundamental geometric loci, auxiliary-triangle indirect elements, 22 interactive .euclid-graphic figures, and 167 graded and review exercises.

Proposition · Commentary

Chapter II: Similitude and Homothecy

Transformational construction and proof via similitude and homothecy (§§ 23–53, Folios 0053–0071, pp. 34–52): homothetic figures, direct and inverse centers of similitude of two circles, the circle of similitude, and the pantograph.

Proposition · Commentary

Chapter III: Properties of the Triangle

Classical synthetic geometry of the triangle (§§ 54–239, Folios 0072–0142, pp. 53–123): harmonic preliminaries, circumcircle and the Problem of Pappus (§§ 54–86), medians and centroid (§§ 87–110), tritangent circles, orthocenter and orthic triangle, Euler line, nine-point circle, Feuerbach’s theorem, and the orthocentric group.

Proposition · Commentary

Chapter IV: The Quadrilateral

Synthetic theory of four-sided figures newly added in the 1952 Second Edition (§§ 240–281, Folios 0143–0158, pp. 124–139): Varignon’s parallelogram, Euler’s diagonal-midpoint identity, cyclic quadrilaterals, Ptolemy’s and Brahmagupta’s theorems, the anticenter, and circumscriptible and bicentric quadrilaterals.

Proposition · Commentary

Chapter V: The Simson Line

Pedal projections from the circumcircle onto the sidelines of a triangle (§§ 282–305, Folios 0159–0169, pp. 140–150): the Wallace–Simson collinearity criterion, bisection of the orthocenter segment on the nine-point circle, and angular intersection laws.

Proposition · Commentary

Chapter VI: Transversals

Directed segments and signed ratio criteria for collinearity and concurrency (§§ 306–344, Folios 0170–0184, pp. 151–165): Euler’s four-point identity, Stewart’s theorem, Menelaus’s transversal theorem, Ceva’s concurrency theorem, Gergonne and Nagel points, and Desargues’s two-triangle theorem.

Proposition · Commentary

Chapter VII: Harmonic Division

Projective and metric theory of harmonic ranges and pencils (§§ 345–357, Folios 0185–0190, pp. 166–171): harmonic conjugates, perspectivity invariance of harmonic pencils, complete quadrangles and quadrilaterals, and the Gauss–Newton midpoint line.

Proposition · Commentary

Chapter VIII: Circles

Comprehensive geometry of one, two, and three circles (§§ 358–517, Folios 0191–0248, pp. 172–229): inverse points, orthogonal circles, poles and polars, Monge’s similitude axes, power and radical axis, coaxal pencils, Casey’s theorem, and Gergonne’s solution to the Problem of Apollonius.

Proposition · Commentary

Chapter IX: Inversion

Conformal transformation by reciprocal radii (§§ 518–546, Folios 0249–0262, pp. 230–243): images of lines and circles, Peaucellier’s straight-line linkage, and inversion proofs of Ptolemy’s theorem, Steiner’s porism, and Feuerbach’s theorem.

Proposition · Commentary

Chapter X: Recent Geometry of the Triangle

Late-nineteenth-century triangle geometry (§§ 547–699, Folios 0263–0313, pp. 244–294): trilinear poles and polars, symmedians and the Lemoine point, Lemoine circles, Apollonian circles and isodynamic points, isogonal conjugates, Brocard points and circle, Tucker circles, and Neuberg’s orthopole.

Proposition · Commentary

Historical and Bibliographical Notes

Altshiller-Court’s historiographical apparatus (Folios 0314–0328, pp. 295–309): abbreviations of foundational treatises and periodicals, article-by-article historical attributions spanning §§ 4–697, and the alphabetical List of Names.

Proposition · Commentary

Index

Alphabetical analytical subject index keyed to §§ 1–699, terminal blank leaf, and the 2007 Dover back cover (Folios 0329–0334, pp. 310–[back cover]).
