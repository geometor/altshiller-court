geometor.altshiller_court.hero_figures¶

Exact symbolic .euclid-graphic hero diagram generator for College Geometry.

Constructs canonical synthetic configurations for Chapters I–X, Frontmatter, Historical Notes, and Section containers using 100% exact SymPy symbolic algebra via geometor.model.Model (auto_intersect=False, zero sp.Float coordinates), verifies their defining theorems deductively (assert_collinear, assert_perpendicular, assert_parallel, assert_concyclic, assert_equal, and cross_ratio), and projects them onto theme-adaptive .euclid-graphic SVGs.
