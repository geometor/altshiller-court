geometor.altshiller_court.codex_items¶

Folio depagination and item-per-page decomposition for College Geometry.

Transforms atomic diplomatic folio files (<NNNN>.rst and <NNNN>.notes.rst) into structured chapter sections, leaf items, and sub-collections (such as the twelve fundamental loci of § 11) with co-located .euclid-graphic SVGs and paired commentary.rst companion tracks.
