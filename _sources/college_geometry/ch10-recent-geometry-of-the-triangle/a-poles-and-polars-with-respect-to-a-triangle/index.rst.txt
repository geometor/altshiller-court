Section A — Poles and Polars with Respect to a Triangle (Trilinear Polar)
=========================================================================

.. rubric:: Chapter X: Recent Geometry of the Triangle • Articles §§ 547–557 (Folios 0263–0266, pp. 244–247)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Triangle ABC with symmedians meeting at Lemoine point K, parallels through K cutting the sides in six concyclic points on the First Lemoine Circle, and Brocard circle on diameter OK
   :caption: Symmedians, Lemoine Point K = X(6), First Lemoine Six-Point Circle, and Brocard Circle

Introduces projective duality relative to a reference triangle (**§§ 547–557**): the :term:`trilinear polar <trilinear-polar>` of a point and the *trilinear pole* of a transversal line.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 547–552 — Harmonic Ceva–Menelaus Pairing**: Given point :math:`P` with cevian traces :math:`X, Y, Z` on :math:`BC, CA, AB`, the harmonic conjugates :math:`X', Y', Z'` of :math:`X, Y, Z` are collinear on a Menelaus transversal—the :term:`trilinear polar <trilinear-polar>` of :math:`P`!
* **§§ 553–557 — Notable Trilinear Polars**: Identifies the line at infinity as the trilinear polar of the :term:`centroid <centroid>` :math:`G`, the orthic axis as the trilinear polar of the :term:`orthocenter <orthocenter>` :math:`H`, and the Lemoine axis of circumcircle tangents as the trilinear polar of the :term:`Lemoine point <lemoine-point>` :math:`K`.

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

In ``geometor/etc``, trilinear polarity sends point :math:`(u : v : w)` directly to the line :math:`x/u + y/v + z/w = 0` in homogeneous trilinear coordinates.
