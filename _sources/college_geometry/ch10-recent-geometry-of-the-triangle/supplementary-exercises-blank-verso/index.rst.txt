Concludes Chapter X across Folios ``0310``–``0313`` (pp. ``291``–``[294]``) with advanced supplementary problems spanning trilinear polarity, Lemoine–Brocard geometry, Tucker circles, and orthopoles, followed by the blank verso leaf ``[294]``.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **Lemoine & Symmedian Synthesis**: Problems linking symmedian chords, tangential triangles, and cosine/Tucker circle radii.
* **Isodynamic & Brocard Configurations**: Metric relations among :math:`\omega`, :math:`OK`, Brocard triangles, and isogonal pedal circles.
* **Orthopole & Simson Loci**: Locus problems as a line rotates about a fixed point or remains tangent to an inscribed circle (Lemoyne's circle theorem).
