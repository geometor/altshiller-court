Section E.b — Brocard Geometry: Brocard Circle (Brocard Triangles, Steiner & Tarry Points)
==========================================================================================

.. rubric:: Chapter X: Recent Geometry of the Triangle • Articles §§ 666–683 (Folios 0298–0303, pp. 279–284)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Triangle ABC with symmedians meeting at Lemoine point K, parallels through K cutting the sides in six concyclic points on the First Lemoine Circle, and Brocard circle on diameter OK
   :caption: Symmedians, Lemoine Point K = X(6), First Lemoine Six-Point Circle, and Brocard Circle

Unites :math:`O, K, \Omega, \Omega'` on the seven-point :term:`Brocard circle <brocard-circle>` (**§§ 666–683**), introducing Brocard's first and second triangles and the :term:`Steiner and Tarry points <steiner-and-tarry-points>`.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 666–675 — The Brocard Circle & Brocard Triangles**: The circle on diameter :math:`OK` passes through :math:`O, K, \Omega, \Omega'` (with :math:`O\Omega = O\Omega'`, :math:`\angle\Omega O\Omega' = 2\omega`), as well as the three vertices of Brocard's first and second triangles!
* **§§ 676–683 — Steiner Point X(99) & Tarry Point X(98)**: Parallels through :math:`A, B, C` to the sides of Brocard's first triangle concur on the circumcircle at the :person:`Steiner <steiner>` point :math:`X(99)`, diametrically opposite the :person:`Tarry <tarry>` point :math:`X(98)` where the corresponding perpendiculars concur.

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------
