Section D — Isogonal Lines (Isogonal Conjugates & Pedal Triangles)
==================================================================

.. rubric:: Chapter X: Recent Geometry of the Triangle • Articles §§ 625–648 (Folios 0286–0293, pp. 267–274)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Acute triangle ABC with altitudes AD, BE, CF meeting at H, highlighted orthic triangle DEF, and circumscribed nine-point circle
   :caption: Orthic Triangle △DEF (Fagnano Minimum-Perimeter Triangle) with Incenter H and Nine-Point Circle

Develops the quadratic Cremona transformation of :term:`isogonal conjugation <isogonal-conjugates>` with respect to :math:`\triangle ABC` (**§§ 625–648**), pairing every interior/exterior point :math:`P` with its reflection-in-bisectors conjugate :math:`P'`.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 625–634 — Existence & Classical Conjugate Pairs**: Reflections of concurrent cevians :math:`AP, BP, CP` across the internal angle bisectors concur at :math:`P'`; incenter/excenters are self-conjugate, :math:`(O, H)` are isogonal conjugates, and :math:`(G, K)` are isogonal conjugates!
* **§§ 635–643 — Shared Pedal Circle Theorem**: The six pedal projections of an isogonal pair :math:`P, P'` onto the three sides lie on a single **pedal circle** centered at the midpoint of :math:`PP'` (specializing for :math:`(O, H)` right back to the :term:`nine-point circle <nine-point-circle>`!).
* **§§ 644–648 — Circumcircle Degeneracy**: Points on the circumcircle have isogonal conjugates at infinity in the direction perpendicular to their :term:`Simson lines <simson-line>`.

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

Corresponds to reciprocal trilinear coordinates :math:`(u : v : w) \mapsto (1/u : 1/v : 1/w)` across ``geometor/etc``.
