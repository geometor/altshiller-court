Section A — The General Quadrilateral
=====================================

.. rubric:: Chapter IV: The Quadrilateral • Articles §§ 240–250 (Folios 0143–0146, pp. 124–127)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Cyclic quadrilateral ABCD inscribed in circle O with diagonals AC, BD and Varignon midpoint parallelogram EFGH
   :caption: Cyclic Quadrilateral ABCD, Ptolemy Diagonals AC, BD, and Varignon Parallelogram EFGH

Opens the 1952 Second Edition's new Chapter IV (**§§ 240–250**) by developing affine and metric properties valid for *any* planar quadrilateral :math:`ABCD`—convex, re-entrant, or crossed.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 240–244 — Varignon's Parallelogram & Bimedians**: The midpoints :math:`E, F, G, H` of the four sides form :person:`Varignon's <varignon>` parallelogram whose sides are parallel to and half the diagonals :math:`AC, BD`, and whose area equals half the area of :math:`ABCD`; the two bimedians and the segment joining the diagonal midpoints bisect one another at the vertex centroid.
* **§§ 245–247 — Euler's Quadrilateral Identity**: The sum of the squares of the four sides equals the sum of the squares of the two diagonals plus four times the square of the segment joining the midpoints of the diagonals (:person:`Euler <euler>` 1748).
* **§§ 248–250 — Area & Orthodiagonal Characterization**: Area via :math:`\tfrac{1}{2} AC \cdot BD \sin\theta`, and proof that :math:`AB^2 + CD^2 = BC^2 + DA^2` holds if and only if :math:`AC \perp BD`.

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

Modeled with exact integer coordinates in the ``cyclic_quadrilateral`` hero diagram, verifying parallelism of opposite Varignon sides symbolically.
