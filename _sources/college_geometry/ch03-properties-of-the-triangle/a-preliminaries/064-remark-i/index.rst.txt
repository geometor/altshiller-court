§ 64. Remark I
==============

.. folio:: 0074
   :page: 55

.. picture:: 0072.g1.svg
   :alt: Fig. 39 — Internal and external division of segment AB in ratio p : q via parallel lines AF and GBH
   :caption: FIG. 39

If :g:`M <0072.g1:M>`, :g:`O <0072.g1:O>` are the midpoints of the segments :g:`AB <0072.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`CD <0072.g1:/ C D />`, we have:

.. math::

   MO = AO - AM = AO - \tfrac{1}{2}\,AB;

hence:

.. math::

   MO = a(p^2 + q^2) : 2(p^2 - q^2).

----
