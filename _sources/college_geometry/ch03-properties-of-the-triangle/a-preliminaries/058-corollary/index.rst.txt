§ 58. Corollary
===============

.. folio:: 0073
   :page: 54

.. enunciation::

   *If* :math:`AB = a` *and* :math:`CD = b`\ *, we have* :math:`b = 2apq : (p^2 - q^2)`\ *.*

Indeed, we have:

.. math::

   AD : AB = p : (p - q), \quad AC : AB = p : (p + q), \quad CD = AD - AC;

hence the announced result.

----
