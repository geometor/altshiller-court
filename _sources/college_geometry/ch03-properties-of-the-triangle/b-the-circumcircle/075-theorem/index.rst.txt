§ 75. Theorem
=============

.. folio:: 0079
   :page: 60

.. enunciation::

   *The difference of the two angles which an* :g:`*internal bisector* <0079.g1:/ A U />` *of an angle of a* :g:`*triangle* <0079.g1:< A B C >>` *makes with the* :g:`*opposite side* <0079.g1:/ B C />` *is equal to the difference of the angles adjacent to this side.*

.. picture:: 0079.g1.svg
   :alt: Fig. 44 — Circumdiameter AK through circumcenter O, altitude AD, bisector AU, and median AA' of triangle ABC
   :caption: FIG. 44

From the two triangles :g:`AUB <0079.g1:< A U B >>`, :g:`AUC <0079.g1:< A U C >>` (Fig. 44) we have:

.. math::

   \angle AUC = B + \tfrac{1}{2}A, \quad \angle AUB = C + \tfrac{1}{2}A;

hence:

.. math::

   \angle AUC - \angle AUB = B - C.

----
