Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Section ``B. THE CIRCUMCIRCLE`` establishes two pillars at once:

1. **The Fundamental Circumradius Datum (§§ 68–69)**: :math:`a = 2R \sin A`, meaning any two of :math:`\{a, A, R\}` determine the third by ruler and compass via the :term:`capable arc <subtended-angle-locus>` centered at the :term:`circumcenter <circumcenter>`.
2. **The South-Pole Property (§ 70)**: The internal angle bisector :math:`AU` of :math:`\angle A` and the perpendicular bisector (:term:`mediator <mediator>`) of :math:`BC` concur at the midpoint :math:`E` of arc :math:`BC` not containing :math:`A` (often called the *South Pole* theorem), creating the similar right triangles :math:`\triangle ADU \sim \triangle EA'U`.
