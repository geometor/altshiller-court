§ 71. Problem
=============

.. folio:: 0077
   :page: 58

.. enunciation::

   *Construct a* :g:`*triangle* <0077.g1:< A B C >>` *given the circumradius, the sum of the lateral sides, and the difference of the base angles* :math:`(R,\; b + c,\; B - C)`\ *.*

.. picture:: 0077.g1.svg
   :alt: Fig. 42 — Isosceles trapezoid ABCK inscribed in circumcircle of triangle ABC with ∠ABK = B − C
   :caption: FIG. 42

Let :g:`ABC <0077.g1:< A B C >>` be the required triangle (Fig. 42). Let the parallel to :g:`BC <0077.g1:[ B C ]>` through :g:`A <0077.g1:A>` meet the circumcircle of :g:`ABC <0077.g1:( A B C )>` in :g:`K <0077.g1:K>`. In the isosceles trapezoid :g:`ABCK <0077.g1:< A B C K >>` we have:

.. math::

   \angle ABK = ABC - KBC = B - BKA = B - BCA = B - C.

The given radius :math:`R` and the known angle :math:`ABK = B - C` determine the segment :g:`AK <0077.g1:/ A K />` (§ 69). Thus in the triangle :g:`ABK <0077.g1:< A B K >>` we know the base :g:`AK <0077.g1:/ A K />`, the opposite angle :g:`B - C <0077.g1:< A B K )>`, and the sum of the sides:

.. math::

   AB + BK = AB + AC = b + c;

hence this :g:`triangle <0077.g1:< A B K >>` may be constructed (§ 17). The parallel to :g:`AK <0077.g1:[ A K ]>` through :g:`B <0077.g1:B>` will meet the :g:`circumcircle <0077.g1:( A B K )>` of :g:`ABK <0077.g1:< A B K >>` in the third vertex :g:`C <0077.g1:C>` of the required triangle :g:`ABC <0077.g1:< A B C >>`.
