Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- **Construction of the Problem of Pappus (§ 84)**: Since the position of the required triangle is not yet fixed on the sides of :math:`\angle BAC`, :person:`Altshiller-Court <altshiller-court>` constructs a congruent auxiliary configuration on an arbitrary segment :math:`B'C' = a` via the capable-arc locus (**§ 11, Locus 7**) and circular secant insertion (**§ 83**), then transfers the angle :math:`\angle B'U'A'` back to the given bisector at :math:`U`. Reflection across the bisector yields the two symmetric solutions.
- **Equal Internal and External Bisectors (Exercises 8–10)**: In :math:`\triangle ABC`, :math:`t_a = t_a'` holds if and only if right triangle :math:`UAU'` is isosceles, i.e. :math:`|B - C| = 90^\circ`, which is equivalent to :math:`AO \parallel BC` (since :math:`\angle BAO = 90^\circ - C`).
- **Circumdiameter Product Formula (§ 85)**: Similar right triangles :math:`\triangle ABD \sim \triangle AKC` (:cite:`euclid:elements` VI.C) immediately yield :math:`bc = 2Rh_a`, one of the cornerstones of classical and modern triangle metric geometry.
