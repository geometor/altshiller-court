§ 72. Problem
=============

.. folio:: 0077
   :page: 58

.. enunciation::

   *Construct a* :g:`*triangle* <0078.g1:< A B C >>` *given the circumradius, the base, and the product of the sum of the other two sides by one of them* :math:`[R,\; a,\; (b + c)b]`\ *.*

Let :g:`ABC <0078.g1:< A B C >>` be the required triangle inscribed in the given :g:`circle <0078.g1:( O )>`, :term:`center <circumcircle>` :g:`O <0078.g1:O>` (Fig. 43). Produce :g:`BA <0078.g1:[ B A ]>` and lay off :eq:`AD = AC <0078.g1:/ D A / = 0078.g1:/ A C />`. We have angle :g:`BDC <0078.g1:< B D C )>` :math:`= \tfrac{1}{2}` :g:`BAC <0078.g1:< B A C )>`, and the latter angle is determined by :math:`R` and :math:`a` (§ 69). Thus if we place the side :math:`BC = a` in the given :g:`circle <0078.g1:( O )>`, radius :math:`R`, we have a locus for :g:`D <0078.g1:D>` (§ 11, :term:`locus 7 <subtended-angle-locus>`).

.. folio:: 0078
   :page: 59

.. picture:: 0078.g1.svg
   :alt: Fig. 43 — Auxiliary triangle DBC with AD = AC and tangent DE from D to circumcircle (O)
   :caption: FIG. 43

Let :g:`DE <0078.g1:/ D E />` be the tangent from :g:`D <0078.g1:D>` to the :g:`circle <0078.g1:( O )>`. We have:

.. math::

   DE^2 = DA \cdot DB = b(b + c),

i.e., we know the length of the tangent :g:`DE <0078.g1:/ D E />` from :g:`D <0078.g1:D>` to the circle :g:`(O) <0078.g1:( O )>`; hence we have a second :term:`locus <geometric-locus>` for :g:`D <0078.g1:D>` (§ 11, locus 2), and the auxiliary triangle :g:`DBC <0078.g1:< D B C >>` may thus be constructed. The :term:`mediator <mediator>` of :g:`CD <0078.g1:/ C D />` meets :g:`BD <0078.g1:[ B D ]>` in the third vertex :g:`A <0078.g1:A>` of the required triangle :g:`ABC <0078.g1:< A B C >>`.
