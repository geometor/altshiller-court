§ 82. Corollary II
==================

.. folio:: 0082
   :page: 63

.. enunciation::

   *The altitude* :g:`AD <0081.g1:/ A D />` *of* :g:`ABC <0081.g1:< A B C >>` *is also an altitude of the triangle* :g:`UAU' <0081.g1:< U A U' >>`\ *; hence:*

   .. math::

      t_a,\; t_a',\; h_a,\; B - C

   *constitute a* :term:`*datum* <datum>` *such that any two of these four magnitudes being given, the remaining two are determined.*
