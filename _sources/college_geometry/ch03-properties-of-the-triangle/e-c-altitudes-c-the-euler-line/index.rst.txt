Section E.c — Altitudes: The Euler Line
=======================================

.. rubric:: Chapter III: Properties of the Triangle • Articles §§ 201–205 (Folios 0120–0121, pp. 101–102)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Triangle ABC with altitudes AD, BE, CF meeting at orthocenter H, reflection H' on circumcircle, and Euler line OGH
   :caption: Altitudes, Orthocenter H = X(4), Circumcircle Reflection H', and Euler Line O–G–H

Establishes :person:`Leonhard Euler's <euler>` 1765 discovery (**§§ 201–205**, :cite:`Euler 1765 <euler:1765>`) that the :term:`circumcenter <circumcircle>` :math:`O`, :term:`centroid <centroid>` :math:`G`, and :term:`orthocenter <orthocenter>` :math:`H` of any non-equilateral triangle are collinear on the :term:`Euler line <euler-line>`.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 201–202 — Homothetic Proof of the Euler Line**: Because :math:`O` is the orthocenter of the complementary triangle :math:`\triangle A'B'C'` and :math:`\mathcal{H}(G, -2)` maps :math:`\triangle A'B'C'` onto :math:`\triangle ABC`, we obtain immediately :math:`AH = 2\,OA'` and :math:`GH = -2\,GO`.
* **§§ 203–205 — Orthocenter–Circumcenter Distance & Reconstruction**: Derives :math:`OH^2 = 9R^2 - (a^2 + b^2 + c^2)` and solves reconstruction problems given subsets of :math:`(O, G, H, A)`.

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

Forms the central spine of Kimberling's ETC (`geometor/etc`): :math:`X(2), X(3), X(4), X(5), X(20), X(30)` all lie on the Euler line with exact integer/rational barycentric combinations.
