Subsequent Articles Roadmap (§§ 89–110)
---------------------------------------

Following the midline theorem (:ref:`§ 87 <ch03-art-087>`) and concurrency of the three medians at the :term:`centroid <centroid>` :math:`G = X(2)` (:ref:`§ 88 <ch03-art-088>`), Section C continues across Folios ``0085``–``0090`` (pp. ``66``–``71``, **§§ 89–110**) to develop the homothetic pair of the :term:`complementary <complementary-triangle>` and :term:`anticomplementary <anticomplementary-triangle>` triangles and the metric laws of medians.

* **§§ 89–95 — Complementary & Anticomplementary Triangles**: The midpoint triangle :math:`A'B'C'` (:term:`complementary triangle <complementary-triangle>`) and parallel-through-vertices triangle (:term:`anticomplementary triangle <anticomplementary-triangle>`) correspond to :math:`\triangle ABC` under :term:`homothecies <homothecy>` :math:`\mathcal{H}(G, -\tfrac{1}{2})` and :math:`\mathcal{H}(G, -2)`.
* **§§ 96–102 — Triangle of Medians & Area Ratio**: Segments parallel and equal to the three medians :math:`m_a, m_b, m_c` form a triangle whose area is :math:`\tfrac{3}{4}` the area of :math:`\triangle ABC`, yielding immediate ruler-and-compass constructions from :math:`(m_a, m_b, m_c)`.
* **§§ 103–110 — Apollonius' Median Theorem & Leibniz Sum**: Establishes :person:`Apollonius' <apollonius>` identity :math:`b^2 + c^2 = 2m_a^2 + \tfrac{1}{2}a^2`, the sum of squares :math:`m_a^2 + m_b^2 + m_c^2 = \tfrac{3}{4}(a^2 + b^2 + c^2)`, and :math:`PA^2 + PB^2 + PC^2 = GA^2 + GB^2 + GC^2 + 3PG^2`.

In ``geometor/etc`` and ``geometor/model``, the centroid :math:`G = X(2) = \tfrac{1}{3}(A + B + C)` has exact rational coordinates over :math:`\mathbb{Q}(A, B, C)` and anchors the :math:`-2 : 1` homothecy driving the :term:`Euler line <euler-line>` and :term:`nine-point circle <nine-point-circle>`.
