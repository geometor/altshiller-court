Section A — Introductory (Directed Segments & Euler's Identity)
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Chapter VI: Transversals • Articles §§ 306–307 (Folios 0170–0171, pp. 151–152)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Triangle ABC with concurrent cevians AX, BY, CZ at Gergonne point Ge and Menelaus transversal UZY
   :caption: Ceva Concurrency at Gergonne Point X(7) and Harmonic Menelaus Transversal

Introduces signed :term:`directed segments <directed-segments>` on an oriented line (**§§ 306–307**, :person:`Möbius <mobius>`, :person:`Chasles <chasles>`), eliminating case-splitting between internal and external points of division.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§ 306 — Chasles's Relation**: For any three collinear points :math:`A, B, C` in any order, :math:`AB + BC + CA = 0` and :math:`AB = -BA`, uniquely encoding a point on :math:`AB` by its signed ratio :math:`AC / CB`.
* **§ 307 — Euler's Four-Point Collinear Identity**: For any four collinear points :math:`A, B, C, D`, signed segments satisfy :math:`AB \cdot CD + AC \cdot DB + AD \cdot BC = 0` (:person:`Euler <euler>`).

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

Corresponds to affine 1D coordinate differences in ``geometor/model`` and underpins signed cross-ratios in ``geometor/pappus``.
