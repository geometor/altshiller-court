§ 21. Definition
================

.. folio:: 0045
   :page: 26

The right triangle :math:`BGH` involves the elements:

.. math::

   b + c, \quad h_b + h_c, \quad A;

hence given any two of these elements the third one is determined. A set of elements of a triangle having this property is sometimes referred to as a :term:`datum`.
