Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

In ``FIG. 21``, right triangle :math:`\triangle BGH` encodes the trigonometric identity :math:`h_b + h_c = (b + c)\sin A` geometrically without algebraic computation: leg :math:`BG = h_b + h_c` lies opposite :math:`\angle BHG = A` (or :math:`180^\circ - A` when :math:`A` is obtuse) on hypotenuse :math:`BH = b + c`. Section 21 isolates this triad :math:`\{b + c, h_b + h_c, A\}` as a *datum* (echoing Euclid's *Data*), immediately reducing any problem containing two of the three quantities to one containing all three.
