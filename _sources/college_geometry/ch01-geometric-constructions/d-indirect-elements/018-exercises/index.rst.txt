§ 18. Exercises (1–6)
=====================

.. folio:: 0043
   :page: 24

Construct a triangle, given:

1. :math:`b - c, a, C`.
2. :math:`b - c, a, B - C`.
3. :math:`b - c, h_b, C`.
4. :math:`b - c, h_b, B - C`.
5. :math:`b - c, A, B`.
6. :math:`b - c, h_b, A`.
