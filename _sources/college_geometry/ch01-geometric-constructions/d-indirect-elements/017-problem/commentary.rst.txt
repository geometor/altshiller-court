Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

In § 15 (completed at the top of this folio), auxiliary triangle :math:`AEF` is constructed from :math:`(2p, 90^\circ + \frac{1}{2}A, h_a)` by intersecting the capable arc on :math:`EF` with the parallel at height :math:`h_a`, yielding two symmetric, one tangent, or zero intersections; vertices :math:`B, C` are then recovered via the :term:`mediators <mediator>` of :math:`AE, AF`. Section 17 applies the same unfolding principle to a single pair of sides: laying off :math:`AD = AC` along :math:`BA` produced creates isosceles :math:`\triangle ACD`, halving the exterior vertical angle so :math:`\angle ADC = \frac{1}{2}A` opposite the known base :math:`BC = a` in auxiliary :math:`\triangle BCD` with side :math:`BD = b + c`.

Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

In § 17's *Discussion*, because :math:`BC = a < b + c = BD` in auxiliary :math:`\triangle BCD`, the known angle :math:`\angle D = \frac{1}{2}A` lies opposite the shorter side :math:`a`, producing the classic SSA (ambiguous case) bifurcation (two, one, or zero triangles depending on :math:`a \gtrless (b + c)\sin\frac{1}{2}A`). Moreover, the identity :math:`\angle BCD = 90^\circ - \frac{1}{2}(B - C)` couples the side-sum :math:`b + c` directly to the angle-difference :math:`B - C`, powering Exercises 6–9 and anticipating Mollweide's formula.
