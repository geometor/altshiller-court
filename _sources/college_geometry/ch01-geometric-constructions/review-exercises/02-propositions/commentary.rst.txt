Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Item 17 (:math:`a - b, h_b + h_c, A`) pairs the *datum* :math:`\{b + c, h_b + h_c, A\}` of § 21 (yielding :math:`b + c`) with :math:`a - b` to give :math:`a + c = (a - b) + (b + c)`, reducing the triangle to two linear conditions and an angle. In ``PROPOSITIONS``, item 29 states van Schooten's theorem (Ptolemy's theorem applied to an equilateral triangle inscribed in a circle: :math:`PA + PB = PC` when :math:`P` lies on minor arc :math:`AB`), and item 30 establishes the area doubling of the parallel-cevian reflection/Steiner-type triangle :math:`XYZ`.

Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Several landmark theorems of modern synthetic geometry appear in embryonic elementary guise on this folio:

- **Item 34**: Routh / equi-ratio cevian-division area ratio :math:`[A'B'C'] / [ABC] = (1 - k + k^2)/(1 + k)^2`.
- **Item 37**: An elementary semicircle instance of :term:`orthocenter` / Pascal-hexagon perpendicularity (:math:`E = CA \cap PB`, :math:`F = AD \cap PC \implies EF \perp AD`).
- **Item 39**: The defining metric invariant of a **harmonic quadrilateral** (:math:`AP : AQ = BP : BQ`, i.e., :math:`AP \cdot BQ = AQ \cdot BP`), anticipating Chapter X's symmedian theory!
- **Item 41**: The tangent-parallel mean-proportional property :math:`BC^2 = BC' \cdot B'C` closely tied to Brocard geometry.

Mathematical & Constructive Commentary

- **Item 55**: Classical Euclidean quadratic identity (*Elements* II.9 and II.10), :math:`AD^2 + DB^2 = 2(AC^2 + CD^2)`, the one-dimensional specialization of Apollonius's median theorem (§ 11, Locus 10).
- **Item 57**: The familiar five-square dissection proving :math:`\text{area}(\text{inner square}) = \frac{1}{5}\,\text{area}(ABCD)`.
- **Item 58**: Blanchet's theorem—for any :term:`cevian` pair :math:`BM, CN` concurring on altitude :math:`AD`, the altitude :math:`AD` bisects :math:`\angle MDN` (a harmonic-bundle projection property revisited in Chapter VII).
