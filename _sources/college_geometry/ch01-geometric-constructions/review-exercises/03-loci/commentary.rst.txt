Mathematical & Constructive Commentary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Chapter I closes with five locus and fixed-point concurrency problems that bridge directly into Chapter II (*Similitude and Homothecy*) and Chapter VIII (*Circles*):

- **Item 60** (on folio ``0051``) yields the circle of similitude (:term:`Apollonian circle <apollonian-circle>` of the :term:`centers of similitude`).
- **Item 64** exhibits stereographic-tangent / :term:`pole-and-polar` linearity (:math:`BM` projects :math:`(O)` onto diameter :math:`AA'`, and :math:`NP = NM` yields a fixed 45° line through :math:`B'`).
- **Item 67** applies independent axial scalings along :math:`OA` and :math:`OB` (:math:`OA'/OA = \lambda`, :math:`OB'/OB = \mu`), mapping the pencil of lines through :math:`P(x_0, y_0)` in oblique coordinates :math:`(OA, OB)` to the pencil through :math:`P'(\lambda x_0, \mu y_0)`.
