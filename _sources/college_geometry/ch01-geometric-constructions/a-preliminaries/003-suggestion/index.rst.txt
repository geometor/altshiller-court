§ 3. Suggestion
===============

.. folio:: 0021
   :page: 2

Most of the preceding problems are stated in conventional symbols. It is instructive to state them in words. For instance, Exercise 4 may be stated as follows: Construct a triangle given the base, the corresponding altitude, and one of the base angles.
