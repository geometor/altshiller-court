Thales of Miletus
=================

*c. 624 – c. 546 BC*

* **Role**: Ionian Natural Philosopher & Geometer
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 11, § 23
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0328 (p. 309) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Founder of deductive Greek geometry; credited with Thales' theorem that a diameter subtends a right angle at any point on the circle (§ 11, Locus 5; Euclid III.31) and proportional intercepts on parallel lines.
