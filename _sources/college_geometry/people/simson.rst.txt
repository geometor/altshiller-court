Simson, Robert
==============

*1687–1768*

* **Role**: Scottish Geometer, University of Glasgow
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 11, § 282, § 308
* **Principal Works**: *Apollonii Pergaei Locorum Planorum Libri II (1749)*; *The Elements of Euclid (1756)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0328 (p. 309) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Restorer of Euclid's Elements (1756), Euclid's Data, and Apollonius's Plane Loci (1749; cited by Altshiller-Court in § 11). Though the 'Simson line' (Ch. V, § 282) bears his name through Poncelet/Servois attribution, it was first published by William Wallace in 1799.
