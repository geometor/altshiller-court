Wallace, William
================

*1768–1843*

* **Role**: Scottish Mathematician, University of Edinburgh
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 282
* **Principal Works**: *Mathematical Repository, Vol. II (1799), p. 111*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0328 (p. 309) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

First published in Thomas Leybourn's Mathematical Repository (1799) the collinearity of the pedal projections of a circumcircle point onto the three sides of a triangle—now known as the Wallace-Simson line (§ 282).
