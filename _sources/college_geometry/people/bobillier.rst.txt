Bobillier, Étienne
==================

*1798–1840*

* **Role**: French Geometer & Mechanician
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 155, § 259
* **Principal Works**: *Mémoires in Annales de Gergonne*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0326 (p. 307) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Early contributor to projective and metric triangle geometry, abbreviated geometry of polars, and pedal/circumcircle properties.
