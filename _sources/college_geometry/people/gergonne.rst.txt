Gergonne, Joseph Diaz
=====================

*1771–1859*

* **Role**: French Mathematician & Founder of Annales de Mathématiques
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 331, § 374, § 421, § 505, § 517
* **Principal Works**: *Annales de mathématiques pures et appliquées (1810–1832)*; *Recherche du cercle qui en touche trois autres sur une sphère ou sur un plan (1816)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0326 (p. 307) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Formulated projective duality, discovered the Gergonne point X(7) where cevians to the incircle contact points concur (§ 331), and devised Gergonne's celebrated master solution to the general Problem of Apollonius using poles, polars, and the radical center (§ 517).
