Catalan, Eugène Charles
=======================

*1814–1894*

* **Role**:  Franco-Belgian Mathematician, University of Liège
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 160, § 290, § 353, § 381, § 496
* **Principal Works**: *Théorèmes et problèmes de géométrie élémentaire (6th ed., 1879)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0326 (p. 307) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Author of Théorèmes et problèmes de géométrie élémentaire (`Cn.` in Altshiller-Court's bibliography), a rich treasury of constructive problems, harmonic division, and coaxal systems.
