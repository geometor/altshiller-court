Johnson, Roger Arthur
=====================

*1889–1954*

* **Role**: American Geometer, Brooklyn College
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 229
* **Principal Works**: *Modern Geometry: An Elementary Treatise on the Geometry of the Triangle and the Circle (1929)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Contemporary of Altshiller-Court and author of Modern Geometry (1929), discoverer of Johnson's three-circle theorem.
