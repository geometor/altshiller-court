La Hire, Philippe de
====================

*1640–1718*

* **Role**: French Geometer & Astronomer
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 292, § 345, § 374
* **Principal Works**: *Sectiones Conicae (1685)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Student of Desargues' projective school; author of Sectiones Conicae (1685), founder of polar reciprocity in the plane, and namesake of De la Hire's Problem (§ 292).
