Ceva, Giovanni
==============

*1647–1734*

* **Role**: Italian Mathematician & Hydraulic Engineer
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 326, § 328
* **Principal Works**: *De lineis rectis se invicem secantibus statica constructio (1678)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0326 (p. 307) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Discovered Ceva's Theorem (1678) governing the concurrency of triangle cevians via products of signed ratios on the sides (Ch. VI, § 326).
