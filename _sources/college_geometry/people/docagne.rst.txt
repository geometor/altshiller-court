d'Ocagne, Philbert Maurice
==========================

*1862–1938*

* **Role**: French Geometer & Founder of Nomography
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 558, § 567, § 568, § 576, § 581, § 599
* **Principal Works**: *Sur un élément du triangle rectiligne: la symédiane (1883)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0326 (p. 307) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Introduced the term symmedian ('symédiane', § 558) and established fundamental properties of symmedians, the Lemoine point, and antiparallels.
