Euler, Leonhard
===============

*1707–1783*

* **Role**: Swiss Mathematician & Natural Philosopher
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 93, § 134, § 152, § 175, § 201, § 207, § 246, § 255, § 307
* **Principal Works**: *Solutio facilis problematum quorumdam geometricorum difficillimorum (1765)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0326 (p. 307) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Initiated modern triangle geometry in his 1765 St. Petersburg memoir: proved the collinearity of the orthocenter H, centroid G, and circumcenter O on the Euler line (§ 201), established OI² = R² - 2Rr (§ 152), Euler's collinear four-point identity (§ 307), and quadrilateral median relations (§ 246).
