Lemoine, Émile Michel Hyacinthe
===============================

*1840–1912*

* **Role**: French Civil Engineer & Geometer
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 136, § 565, § 572, § 573, § 576, § 588, § 591, § 593, § 616
* **Principal Works**: *Note sur un point remarquable du plan du triangle (1873)*; *La Géométrographie ou l'art des constructions géométriques (1902)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Sparked the modern geometry of the triangle with his 1873 Lyon paper on the symmedian (Lemoine) point K = X(6), Lemoine circles (first and cosine circles), Lemoine axis, and geometrography (quantitative measure of construction simplicity, Ch. X, §§ 558–596).
