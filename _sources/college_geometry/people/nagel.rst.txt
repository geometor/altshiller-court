Nagel, Christian Heinrich von
=============================

*1803–1882*

* **Role**: German Geometer (Ulm)
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 160, § 196, § 333, § 626
* **Principal Works**: *Untersuchungen über die wichtigsten zum Dreiecke gehörigen Kreise (1836)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Investigated the tritangent centers, the Nagel point X(8) where cevians to the excircle contact points concur (§ 333), and isogonal/isotomic relations (1836).
