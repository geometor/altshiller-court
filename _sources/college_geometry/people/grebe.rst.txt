Grebe, Ernst Wilhelm
====================

*1804–1874*

* **Role**: German Mathematician
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 572, § 573
* **Principal Works**: *Das geradlinige Dreieck in Bezug auf die Quadrate der Perpendikel (1847)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0326 (p. 307) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Studied the symmedian point in 1847 (hence called the Grebe point in German literature, § 572).
