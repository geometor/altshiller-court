Desargues, Girard
=================

*1591–1661*

* **Role**: French Architect & Founder of Projective Geometry
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 343, § 345, § 374
* **Principal Works**: *Brouillon project d'une atteinte aux événemens des rencontres du cône avec un plan (1639)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0326 (p. 307) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Introduced points and lines at infinity, involution, polar reciprocity, and Desargues' Two-Triangle Perspective Theorem (§ 343).
