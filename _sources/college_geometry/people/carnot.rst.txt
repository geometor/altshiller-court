Carnot, Lazare Nicolas Marguerite
=================================

*1753–1823*

* **Role**: French Mathematician & Statesman ('Organizer of Victory')
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 133, § 146, § 175, § 185, § 224, § 310, § 314
* **Principal Works**: *Géométrie de position (1803)*; *Essai sur la théorie des transversales (1806)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0326 (p. 307) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Pioneered the systematic use of signed geometric quantities and transversals in Géométrie de position (1803); proved Carnot's Theorem relating circumradius, inradius, and signed distances from circumcenter to triangle sides (§ 146).
