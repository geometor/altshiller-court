Petersen, Julius Peter Christian
================================

*1839–1910*

* **Role**: Danish Mathematician
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 4, § 11, § 15, § 23, § 31
* **Principal Works**: *Methods and Theories for the Solution of Problems of Geometrical Constructions (1879)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0026 (p. 7) <ch01-art-008>` · :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Author of Methods and Theories for the Solution of Problems of Geometrical Constructions (`Pt.`, 1866; English ed. 1879), the direct methodological ancestor of Altshiller-Court's Chapter I and Chapter II (Method of Loci, Similitude, Rotation, Translation).
