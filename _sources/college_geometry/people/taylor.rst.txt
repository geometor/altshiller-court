Taylor, Henry Martyn
====================

*1842–1927*

* **Role**: English Mathematician, Trinity College, Cambridge
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 689
* **Principal Works**: *On a Six-Point Circle Connected with a Triangle (Messenger of Mathematics, 1882)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0328 (p. 309) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Investigated the six projections of the feet of the altitudes onto the other two sides, proving they lie on a special Tucker circle: the Taylor circle (§ 689).
