Stewart, Matthew
================

*1717–1785*

* **Role**: Scottish Geometer, University of Edinburgh
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 308
* **Principal Works**: *Some General Theorems of Considerable Use in the Higher Parts of Mathematics (1746)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Student of Robert Simson and Colin Maclaurin; published Stewart's Theorem (1746) relating the length of any cevian to the side lengths and divided base segments (Ch. VI.B, § 308).
