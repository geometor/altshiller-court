Rouché, Eugène
==============

*1832–1910*

* **Role**: French Mathematician
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 84, § 215, § 310, § 440, § 506, § 558, § 650, § 677
* **Principal Works**: *Traité de géométrie (with Ch. de Comberousse, 7th ed., 1900)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Co-author with Charles de Comberousse of Traité de géométrie (`Rouché.`, 7th ed. 1900, with Neuberg's appendix on triangle geometry), Altshiller-Court's single most frequently cited French reference.
