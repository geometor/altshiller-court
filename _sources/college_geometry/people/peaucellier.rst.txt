Peaucellier, Charles-Nicolas
============================

*1832–1913*

* **Role**: French Military Engineer
* **Articles in College Geometry**: § 518, § 533
* **Principal Works**: *Note sur le compas composé (Nouvelles Annales, 1864 / 1873)*
* **Edition Loci**: :ref:`f. 0327 (p. 308) <historical-and-bibliographical-notes-names-list-of-names>`

Invented Peaucellier's Cell (1864), the first exact planar linkage converting circular motion into rectilinear motion via geometric inversion (§ 533).
