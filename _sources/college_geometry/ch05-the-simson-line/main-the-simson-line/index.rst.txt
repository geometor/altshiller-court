The Simson Line (Wallace-Simson Theorems & Exercises)
=====================================================

.. rubric:: Chapter V: The Simson Line • Articles §§ 282–305 (Folios 0159–0169, pp. 140–150)

.. picture:: hero.svg
   :alt: Triangle ABC with circumcircle point P, orthogonal projections X, Y, Z collinear on the Wallace-Simson line, and midpoint M of PH

Develops the complete synthetic theory of the :term:`Wallace–Simson line <simson-line>` (**§§ 282–305**, Folios ``0159``–``0169``, pp. ``140``–``150``), discovered by :person:`William Wallace <wallace>` in 1799 and traditionally named after :person:`Robert Simson <simson>`.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities
----------------------------------------------

* **§§ 282–287 — Wallace–Simson Collinearity Criterion**: The feet :math:`X, Y, Z` of the perpendiculars dropped from a point :math:`P` onto the sidelines :math:`BC, CA, AB` of :math:`\triangle ABC` are collinear if and only if :math:`P` lies on the :term:`circumcircle <circumcircle>` of :math:`\triangle ABC`.
* **§§ 288–297 — Angular Rotation & Perpendicular Simson Lines**: As :math:`P` traverses the circumcircle, its Simson line rotates at half the angular rate in the *opposite* sense; the Simson lines of two diametrically opposite points :math:`P, P'` are mutually perpendicular.
* **§§ 298–305 — Bisection of PH on the Nine-Point Circle**: The Simson line of :math:`P` bisects the segment :math:`PH` joining :math:`P` to the :term:`orthocenter <orthocenter>` :math:`H` at a point :math:`M` on the :term:`nine-point circle <nine-point-circle>`, and two diametral Simson lines intersect right on the nine-point circle (:person:`Steiner's <steiner>` three-cusped hypocycloid envelope).

Constructive & Symbolic Connections
-----------------------------------

Verified symbolically in the ``simson_line`` codex hero model (:math:`P = (4, 12)` on the :math:`13\text{–}14\text{–}15` circumcircle), proving collinearity of :math:`X, Y, Z, M` and concyclicity of :math:`M` with the nine-point circle.
