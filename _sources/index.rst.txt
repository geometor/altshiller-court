=======================
Nathan Altshiller-Court
=======================

The mission of this project is to capture a semantic digital library of the works of :person:`Nathan Altshiller-Court <altshiller-court>` (1881–1968), Professor of Mathematics at the University of Oklahoma and author of landmark treatises in modern synthetic geometry of the triangle and circle. Within the interconnected `GEOMETOR <https://geometor.com/books/>`_ semantic digital archive, this work is liberated from static print bounds—decomposed into addressable semantic units, calibrated vector diagrams, primary-source folio provenance, and cross-corpus citations speaking one shared geometric language.
