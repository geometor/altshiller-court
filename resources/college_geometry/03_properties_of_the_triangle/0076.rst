B. THE CIRCUMCIRCLE
-------------------

.. enunciation::

   **68. Theorem.** *If a triangle inscribed in a given* :term:`*circle* <circumcircle>` *has a constant angle, then the opposite side is tangent to a fixed circle concentric with the given circle.*

The proposition may be considered as the converse of :term:`locus 7 <subtended-angle-locus>` (§ 11). The proof is left to the student.
