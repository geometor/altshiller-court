.. enunciation::

   **60. Problem.** *Given three collinear points* :g:`A <0072.g1:A>`\ *,* :g:`B <0072.g1:B>`\ *,* :g:`C <0072.g1:C>`\ *, construct the* :term:`*harmonic conjugate* <harmonic-division>` :g:`D <0072.g1:D>` *of* :g:`C <0072.g1:C>` *with respect to the points* :g:`A <0072.g1:A>`\ *,* :g:`B <0072.g1:B>`\ *.*

Through the points :g:`A <0072.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0072.g1:B>` draw any pair of parallel lines :g:`AF <0072.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`GBH <0072.g1:[ G B H ]>` (Fig. 39) and through :g:`C <0072.g1:C>` any transversal meeting :g:`AF <0072.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`BG <0072.g1:[ B G ]>` in :g:`F <0072.g1:F>`, :g:`G <0072.g1:G>`. On :g:`GB <0072.g1:[ G B ]>` lay off :eq:`BH = BG <0072.g1:/ B H / = 0072.g1:/ B G />`. The line :g:`FH <0072.g1:[ F H ]>` meets :g:`AB <0072.g1:[ A B ]>` in the required point :g:`D <0072.g1:D>`.

.. enunciation::

   **61. Theorem.** *The feet of the two pairs of perpendiculars dropped upon a given line from two pairs of* :term:`*harmonic points* <harmonic-division>` *are also two pairs of harmonic points.*

Indeed, the perpendiculars are four parallel lines; hence the segments determined by their feet are to each other as the corresponding segments determined by the two given pairs of harmonic points.

.. enunciation::

   **62. Theorem.** *If the points* :g:`C <0072.g1:C>`\ *,* :g:`D <0072.g1:D>` *divide the segment* :g:`AB <0072.g1:/ A B />` *harmonically in the ratio* :math:`p : q`\ *, the midpoint* :g:`O <0072.g1:O>` *of the segment* :g:`CD <0072.g1:/ C D />` *divides the segment* :g:`AB <0072.g1:/ A B />` *externally in the ratio* :math:`p^2 : q^2`\ *.*

The point :g:`O <0072.g1:O>` lies outside the segment :g:`AB <0072.g1:/ A B />` (§ 56). Now:

.. math::

   AO = \tfrac{1}{2}\,CD + AC, \quad BO = \tfrac{1}{2}\,CD - BC.

Substituting for :g:`CD <0072.g1:/ C D />`, :g:`AC <0072.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`BC <0072.g1:/ C B />` their values (§§ 57, 58), we obtain:

.. math::

   AO = ap^2 : (p^2 - q^2), \quad BO = aq^2 : (p^2 - q^2);

hence the announced relation.

.. enunciation::

   **63. Corollary.** *We have (§ 58):*

   .. math::

      OA \cdot OB = OC^2.

**64. Remark I.** If :g:`M <0072.g1:M>`, :g:`O <0072.g1:O>` are the midpoints of the segments :g:`AB <0072.g1:/ A B />`, :g:`CD <0072.g1:/ C D />`, we have:

.. math::

   MO = AO - AM = AO - \tfrac{1}{2}\,AB;

hence:

.. math::

   MO = a(p^2 + q^2) : 2(p^2 - q^2).

**65. Remark II.** The point :g:`M <0072.g1:M>` lies outside the segment :g:`CD <0072.g1:/ C D />` (§ 56).

.. enunciation::

   **66. Theorem.** *The sum of the squares of two harmonic segments is equal to four times the square of the distance between the midpoints of these segments.*

We have (§ 58):

.. math::

   AB^2 + CD^2 = a^2 + 4a^2p^2q^2 : (p^2 - q^2)^2 = a^2(p^2 + q^2)^2 : (p^2 - q^2)^2;

hence (§ 64):

.. math::

   AB^2 + CD^2 = 4\,MO^2.
