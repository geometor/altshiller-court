.. enunciation::

   **82. Corollary II.** *The altitude* :g:`AD <0081.g1:/ A D />` *of* :g:`ABC <0081.g1:< A B C >>` *is also an altitude of the triangle* :g:`UAU' <0081.g1:< U A U' >>`\ *; hence:*

   .. math::

      t_a,\; t_a',\; h_a,\; B - C

   *constitute a* :term:`*datum* <datum>` *such that any two of these four magnitudes being given, the remaining two are determined.*

.. enunciation::

   **83. Problem.** *Through the midpoint* :g:`E <0082.g1:E>` *of the arc* :math:`BEC` *of a* :g:`*circle* <0082.g1:( B E C )>` *to draw a* :g:`*secant* <0082.g1:[ E U A ]>` *meeting the chord* :g:`BC <0082.g1:/ B C />` *in* :g:`U <0082.g1:U>` *and the circle again in* :g:`A <0082.g1:A>`\ *, so that* :g:`AU <0082.g1:/ A U />` *shall have a given length,* :math:`t`\ *.*

.. picture:: 0082.g1.svg
   :alt: Fig. 46 — Secant EUA through arc midpoint E meeting chord BC in U with intercepted segment AU = t
   :caption: FIG. 46

Let the diameter :g:`EMF <0082.g1:[ E M F ]>` (Fig. 46) passing through :g:`E <0082.g1:E>` meet the chord :g:`BC <0082.g1:/ B C />` in :g:`M <0082.g1:M>`. From the two equiangular right triangles :g:`EUM <0082.g1:< E U M >>`, :g:`EFA <0082.g1:< E F A >>` we have:

.. math::

   EF : EU = EA : EM.

Now if we put:

.. math::

   EF = 2R, \quad EM = m, \quad EU = x, \quad EA = x + t,

the proportion may be written:

.. math::

   2R \cdot m = x(x + t),

and the segment :math:`x` may be constructed (§ 67).

The known length of the segment :g:`EU <0082.g1:/ E U />` determines the point :g:`U <0082.g1:U>`; hence the required secant :g:`EUA <0082.g1:[ E U A ]>`.

.. enunciation::

   **84. The Problem of Pappus.** *Through a given point on the bisector of a given angle to draw a secant on which the given angle shall intercept a segment of given length.*
