Let :g:`DE <0078.g1:/ D E />` be the tangent from :g:`D <0078.g1:D>` to the :g:`circle <0078.g1:( O )>`. We have:

.. math::

   DE^2 = DA \cdot DB = b(b + c),

i.e., we know the length of the tangent :g:`DE <0078.g1:/ D E />` from :g:`D <0078.g1:D>` to the circle :g:`(O) <0078.g1:( O )>`; hence we have a second :term:`locus <geometric-locus>` for :g:`D <0078.g1:D>` (§ 11, locus 2), and the auxiliary triangle :g:`DBC <0078.g1:< D B C >>` may thus be constructed. The :term:`mediator <mediator>` of :g:`CD <0078.g1:/ C D />` meets :g:`BD <0078.g1:[ B D ]>` in the third vertex :g:`A <0078.g1:A>` of the required triangle :g:`ABC <0078.g1:< A B C >>`.

EXERCISES
~~~~~~~~~

Construct a triangle, given:

1. :math:`R,\; A,\; b^2 + c^2`.
2. :math:`R,\; A,\; b : c`.
3. :math:`R,\; a,\; h_b`.
4. :math:`R,\; a,\; b + c`.
5. :math:`R,\; b,\; B - C`.
6. :math:`R,\; m_b,\; C`.
7. :math:`R,\; A,\; 2p`.
8. :math:`R,\; A,\; a - b`.
9. :math:`R,\; b + c,\; h_b + h_c`.
10. :math:`R,\; b - c,\; h_c - h_b`.
11. :math:`R,\; A,\; h_b + h_c`.
12. :math:`R,\; a,\; B - C`.
13. :math:`R,\; b,\; a : h_a`.
14. :math:`a,\; A,\; b(b + c)`.
15. :math:`R,\; b - c,\; B - C`.

.. enunciation::

   **73. Theorem.** *The* :g:`*angle* <0079.g1:< D A K )>` *between the* :g:`*circumdiameter* <0079.g1:/ A K />` *and the* :g:`*altitude* <0079.g1:/ A D />` *issued from the same* :g:`*vertex* <0079.g1:A>` *of a* :g:`*triangle* <0079.g1:< A B C >>` *(a) is equal to the difference of the other two angles of the triangle, and (b) is bisected by the* :g:`*bisector* <0079.g1:/ A U />` *of the angle of the triangle at the vertex considered.*
