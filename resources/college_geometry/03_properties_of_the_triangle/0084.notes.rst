- **Circumradius–Area Formula (§ 86)**: Multiplying :math:`bc = 2Rh_a` (§ 85) by :math:`a` yields :math:`abc = 4RS`, linking the :term:`circumcircle <circumcircle>` radius :math:`R` directly to the side product and area :math:`S` (compare :person:`Heron <heron>`'s formula in § 132).
