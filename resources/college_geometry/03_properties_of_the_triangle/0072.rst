A. PRELIMINARIES

.. enunciation::

   **54. Problem.** *Divide a given segment,* :g:`AB <0072.g1:/ A B />`\ *, internally and externally in a given ratio* :math:`p : q`\ *.*

Through the ends :g:`A <0072.g1:A>`, :g:`B <0072.g1:B>` (Fig. 39) of the given segment draw any pair of parallel lines :g:`AF <0072.g1:/ A F />`, :g:`GBH <0072.g1:[ G B H ]>`, and lay off :math:`AF = p`, :eq:`BG = BH <0072.g1:/ B G / = 0072.g1:/ B H />` :math:`= q`.

.. assertion::

   The lines :g:`FG <0072.g1:[ F G ]>`, :g:`FH <0072.g1:[ F H ]>` meet the line :g:`AB <0072.g1:[ A B ]>` in the required points :g:`C <0072.g1:C>`, :g:`D <0072.g1:D>`, as is readily seen from the two pairs of similar triangles :g:`CAF <0072.g1:< C A F >>` and :g:`CBG <0072.g1:< C B G >>`, :g:`DAF <0072.g1:< D A F >>` and :g:`DBH <0072.g1:< D B H >>`.
