C. MEDIANS
----------

.. enunciation::

   **87. Theorem.** *\(a) The line joining the midpoints of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side and is equal to one-half its length.*

   *\(b) The line drawn parallel to a side of a triangle through the midpoint of another side passes through the midpoint of the third side.*

.. enunciation::

   **88. Theorem.** *The three medians of a* :g:`*triangle* <0084.g1:< A B C >>` *meet in a* :g:`*point* <0084.g1:G>`\ *, and each median is trisected by this point.*

.. picture:: 0084.g1.svg
   :alt: Fig. 47 — Triangle ABC with medians AA', BB', CC' concurring at centroid G in ratio 2 : 1, midline C'B', and auxiliary points M, N, K
   :caption: FIG. 47

Let :g:`G <0084.g1:G>` be the point of intersection of the two medians :g:`BB' <0084.g1:/ B B' />`, :g:`CC' <0084.g1:/ C C' />` (Fig. 47).

From the two similar triangles :g:`GBC <0084.g1:< G B C >>`, :g:`GB'C' <0084.g1:< G B' C' >>` we have:

.. math::

   BG : B'G = CG : C'G = BC : B'C' = 2 : 1.

Thus any two medians trisect each other, which proves the proposition.
