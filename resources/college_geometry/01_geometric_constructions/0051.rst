48. :math:`MNP, M'N'P'` are two tangents to the same circle :math:`PQP'`, and :math:`AM, BN, AM', BN'` are perpendiculars to them respectively from the two given points :math:`A, B`. If :math:`MP : PN = M'P' : P'N'`, prove that the two tangents are parallel.
49. :math:`ABC` is a triangle inscribed in a circle; :math:`DE` is the diameter bisecting :math:`BC` at :math:`G`; from :math:`E` a perpendicular :math:`EK` is drawn to one of the sides, and the perpendicular from the vertex :math:`A` on :math:`DE` meets :math:`DE` in :math:`H`. Show that :math:`EK` touches the circle :math:`GHK`.
50. If the internal bisector of an angle of a triangle is equal to one of the including sides, show that the projection of the other side upon this bisector is equal to half the sum of the sides considered.
51. From two points, one on each of two opposite sides of a parallelogram, lines are drawn to the opposite vertices. Prove that the straight line through the points of intersection of these lines bisects the area of the parallelogram.
52. The point :math:`B` being the midpoint of the segment :math:`AC`, the circle :math:`(A, AB)` is drawn, and upon an arbitrary tangent to this circle the perpendicular :math:`CD` is dropped from the point :math:`C`. Show that angle :math:`ABD = 3\,BDC`.
53. Show that the line of centers of the two circles inscribed in the two right triangles into which a given right triangle is divided by the altitude to the hypotenuse is equal to the distance from the :term:`incenter <incircle>` of the given triangle to the vertex of its right angle.
54. :math:`ABC` is an equilateral triangle, :math:`D` a point on :math:`BC` such that :math:`BD` is one-third of :math:`BC`, and :math:`E` is a point on :math:`AB` equidistant from :math:`A` and :math:`D`. Show that :math:`CE = EB + BD`.
55. If a line :math:`AB` is bisected by :math:`C` and divided by :math:`D` unequally internally or externally, prove that :math:`AD^2 + DB^2 = 2(AC^2 + CD^2)`.
56. Let :math:`M` be the midpoint of chord :math:`AB` of a circle, center :math:`O`; on :math:`OM` as diameter draw another circle, and at any point :math:`T` of this circle draw a tangent to it meeting the outer circle in :math:`E`. Prove that :math:`AE^2 + BE^2 = 4\,ET^2`.
57. If :math:`M, N, P, Q` are the midpoints of the sides :math:`AB, BC, CD, DA` of a square :math:`ABCD`, prove that the intersections of the lines :math:`AN, BP, CQ, DM` determine a square of area one-fifth that of the given square :math:`ABCD`.
58. If :math:`M, N` are points on the sides :math:`AC, AB` of a triangle :math:`ABC` and the lines :math:`BM, CN` intersect on the altitude :math:`AD`, show that :math:`AD` is the bisector of the angle :math:`MDN`.

59. :math:`A, B, C, D` are fixed points on a circle :math:`(O)`. The lines joining :math:`C, D` to a variable point :math:`P` meet :math:`(O)` again in :math:`Q, R`. Find the :term:`locus <geometric-locus>` of the second point of intersection :math:`S` of the two circles :math:`PQB, PRA`.
60. Find the :term:`locus <geometric-locus>` of a point at which two given circles subtend equal angles.
61. Given two points :math:`A, B`, collinear with the center :math:`O` of a given circle, and a variable diameter :math:`PQ` of this circle, find the :term:`locus <geometric-locus>` of the second point of intersection of the two circles :math:`APO, BQO`.
62. On the sides :math:`OA, OB` of a given angle :math:`O` two variable points :math:`A', B'` are marked so that the ratio :math:`AA' : BB'` is constant, and on the segment :math:`A'B'` the point :math:`I` is marked so that the ratio :math:`A'I : B'I` is constant. Prove that the :term:`locus <geometric-locus>` of the point :math:`I` is a straight line.
