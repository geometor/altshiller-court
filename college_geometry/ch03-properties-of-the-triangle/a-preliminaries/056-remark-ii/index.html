§ 56. Remark II¶

If \(p > q\), we have \(AC > CB\) and \(AD > DB\), so that the midpoint M of AB lies outside the segment CD. The same relative position of the points M, C, D prevails, if \(p < q\).

If \(p = q\), the point C coincides with M, and there is no external point of division, since FH is then parallel to AB.

See Commentary on § 56. Remark II.
