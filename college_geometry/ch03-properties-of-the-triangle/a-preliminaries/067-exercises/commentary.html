Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

Section B. THE CIRCUMCIRCLE establishes two pillars at once:

  1. The Fundamental Circumradius Datum (§§ 68–69): \(a = 2R \sin A\), meaning any two of \(\{a, A, R\}\) determine the third by ruler and compass via the capable arc centered at the circumcenter.

  2. The South-Pole Property (§ 70): The internal angle bisector \(AU\) of \(\angle A\) and the perpendicular bisector (mediator) of \(BC\) concur at the midpoint \(E\) of arc \(BC\) not containing \(A\) (often called the South Pole theorem), creating the similar right triangles \(\triangle ADU \sim \triangle EA'U\).
