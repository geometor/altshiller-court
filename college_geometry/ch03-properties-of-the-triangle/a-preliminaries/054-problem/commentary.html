Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

Problem § 54 supplies the canonical ruler-and-compass construction promised in § 2 for dividing a segment \(AB\) both internally at \(C\) and externally at \(D\) in the same ratio \(p : q\) by laying off parallel directed intercepts \(AF = p\) and \(BG = BH = q\). Because \((A, B; C, D)\) is a harmonic range (§ 59), FIG. 39 also marks the midpoint \(M\) of \(AB\) (tick mark) and the midpoint \(O\) of \(CD\) (dot), preparing the metric harmonic theorems of §§ 62–66.
