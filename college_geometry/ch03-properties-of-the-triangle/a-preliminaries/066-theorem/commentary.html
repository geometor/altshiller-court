Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

Three foundational metric characterizations of harmonic ranges appear on this page:

  1. Newton’s Relation (§ 63): \(OA \cdot OB = OC^2 = OD^2\), showing that \(A\) and \(B\) are inverse points with respect to the circle on diameter \(CD\)—and since \(OA : OB = p^2 : q^2\) (§ 62), this circle is precisely the Apollonian circle of locus ratio \(p : q\).

  2. Orthogonality Invariant (§ 66): Writing \(AB = 2r_1\) and \(CD = 2r_2\), the identity \(AB^2 + CD^2 = 4\,MO^2\) becomes \(r_1^2 + r_2^2 = MO^2\), proving that the circles on diameters \(AB\) and \(CD\) are orthogonal circles (§ 368).
