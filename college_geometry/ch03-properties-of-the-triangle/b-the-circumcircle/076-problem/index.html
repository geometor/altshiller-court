§ 76. Problem¶

Construct a *triangle* given the *altitude*, the *median*, and the *bisector* issued from the same *vertex* \((h_a,\; m_a,\; t_a)\).
f. 0080 · p. 61

The two right triangles having for their common leg \(AD = h_a\) (Fig. 44) and for hypotenuses \(AA' = m_a\) and \(AU = t_a\) may be constructed. The circumcenter O of the required triangle ABC lies on the perpendicular to DA' at A' and on the line which makes with AU an angle equal to the angle DAU (§ 73b); hence O may be determined. The circle (O, OA) meets the line DA' in the two vertices B, C of the required triangle ABC.

See Commentary on § 76. Problem.
