Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

  • Construction of the Problem of Pappus (§ 84): Since the position of the required triangle is not yet fixed on the sides of \(\angle BAC\), Altshiller-Court constructs a congruent auxiliary configuration on an arbitrary segment \(B'C' = a\) via the capable-arc locus (§ 11, Locus 7) and circular secant insertion (§ 83), then transfers the angle \(\angle B'U'A'\) back to the given bisector at \(U\). Reflection across the bisector yields the two symmetric solutions.

  • Equal Internal and External Bisectors (Exercises 8–10): In \(\triangle ABC\), \(t_a = t_a'\) holds if and only if right triangle \(UAU'\) is isosceles, i.e. \(|B - C| = 90^\circ\), which is equivalent to \(AO \parallel BC\) (since \(\angle BAO = 90^\circ - C\)).

  • Circumdiameter Product Formula (§ 85): Similar right triangles \(\triangle ABD \sim \triangle AKC\) (euclid:elements VI.C) immediately yield \(bc = 2Rh_a\), one of the cornerstones of classical and modern triangle metric geometry.
