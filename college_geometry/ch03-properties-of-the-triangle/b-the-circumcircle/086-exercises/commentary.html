Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

  • Circumradius–Area Formula (§ 86): Multiplying \(bc = 2Rh_a\) (§ 85) by \(a\) yields \(abc = 4RS\), linking the circumcircle radius \(R\) directly to the side product and area \(S\) (compare Heron’s formula in § 132). Exercise 4 states a classic Pappus-type locus equality on the circumcircle relative to \(\triangle ABC\) and its tangential triangle.

  • Medians and the Centroid (§§ 87–88): Using the midline theorem (euclid:elements VI.2, \(B'C' \parallel BC\) and \(B'C' = \tfrac{1}{2}BC\)), Altshiller-Court proves \(\triangle GBC \sim \triangle GB'C'\) with ratio \(2 : 1\), establishing the concurrency of the three medians at the centroid \(G = X(2)\) (kimberling:etc; Archimedes, On the Equilibrium of Planes I.14). Notice that FIG. 47 already includes auxiliary midpoints \(M, N\) of \(BG, CG\) and point \(K\) on \(AA'\) produced (used in the alternative parallelogram proofs and exercises on p. 66).
