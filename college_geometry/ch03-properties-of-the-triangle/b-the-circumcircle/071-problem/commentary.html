Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

Problem § 71 illustrates the power of inscribing an auxiliary isosceles trapezoid \(ABCK\) (\(AK \parallel BC\)) inside the circumcircle: reflecting \(A\) across the perpendicular bisector of \(BC\) to \(K\) converts the indirect elements \(b + c\) and \(B - C\) directly into \(AB + BK\) and \(\angle ABK\) in \(\triangle ABK\), reducing the problem immediately to Chapter I, § 17!
