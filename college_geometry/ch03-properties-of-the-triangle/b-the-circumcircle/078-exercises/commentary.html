Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

Both § 76 \((h_a, m_a, t_a)\) and § 78 \((a, m_a, B - C)\) construct the circumcenter \(O\) directly before finding \(B\) and \(C\)! In § 76, right triangles \(ADA'\) and \(ADU\) fix \(A, D, U, A'\), and \(O\) is the intersection of the perpendicular to \(BC\) at \(A'\) with the isogonal ray of \(AD\) across \(AU\). In § 78, intersecting chords \(A'A \cdot A'J = A'B \cdot A'C = \tfrac{1}{4}a^2\) determine the chord extension \(A'J = a^2 / (4m_a)\), so \(O\) is determined as the intersection of the mediator of \(AJ\) with the capable arc \(\angle AOA' = 180^\circ - (B - C)\).
