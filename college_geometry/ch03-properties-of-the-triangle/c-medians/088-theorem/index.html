§ 88. Theorem¶

The three medians of a *triangle* meet in a *point*, and each median is trisected by this point.

Let G be the point of intersection of the two medians BB', CC' (Fig. 47).

From the two similar triangles GBC, GB'C' we have:

\[BG : B'G = CG : C'G = BC : B'C' = 2 : 1.\]

Thus any two medians trisect each other, which proves the proposition.

See Commentary on § 88. Theorem.
