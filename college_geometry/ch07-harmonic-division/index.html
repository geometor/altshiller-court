Chapter VII: Harmonic Division¶

Harmonic Conjugates, Metric Relations, Harmonic Pencils, Complete Quadrangle and Complete Quadrilateral

Projective and metric theory of harmonic ranges and pencils (§§ 345–357, Folios 0185–0190, pp. 166–171): harmonic conjugates, perspectivity invariance of harmonic pencils, complete quadrangles and quadrilaterals, and the Gauss–Newton midpoint line.

Harmonic Ranges, Pencils, and Complete Quadrangles

Elevates harmonic division (§§ 345–357, Folios 0185–0190, pp. 166–171) from a metric segment ratio to the fundamental projective invariant of harmonic pencils, complete quadrangles, and complete quadrilaterals (Pappus VII, Chasles).

See Commentary on Chapter VII: Harmonic Division.
