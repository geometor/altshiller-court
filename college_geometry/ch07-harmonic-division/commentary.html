Commentary on Chapter VII: Harmonic Division¶

Back to Chapter VII: Harmonic Division.

Constructive Analysis, Historical Provenance & GEOMETOR Notes (0185–0190, pp. 166–171)

Mathematical Architecture & Projective Core¶

Chapter VII (§§ 345–357, pp. 166–171) elevates harmonic division from a metric ratio on a single segment to a projective invariant of pencils and quadrangles:

  • Metric Characterizations (§§ 345–349): Equivalent algebraic forms \(\frac{2}{AB} = \frac{1}{AC} + \frac{1}{AD}\) and \(OB^2 = OC \cdot OD\) (where \(O\) is the midpoint of \(AB\)), linking harmonic conjugates directly to inverse points in Chapter VIII.

  • Harmonic Pencils (§§ 350–353): Projective invariance of \(O(ABCD) = -1\) under arbitrary transversal sections (Pappus, Collection VII; Chasles, Traité).

  • Complete Quadrangle & Complete Quadrilateral (§§ 354–357): Straightedge-only construction of the fourth harmonic point via the diagonal triangle of a complete quadrilateral, including the Gauss–Newton midpoint line (§ 353).
