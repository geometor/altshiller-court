Section E.b — Brocard Geometry: Brocard Circle (Brocard Triangles, Steiner & Tarry Points)¶

Chapter X: Recent Geometry of the Triangle • Articles §§ 666–683 (Folios 0298–0303, pp. 279–284)

Unites \(O, K, \Omega, \Omega'\) on the seven-point Brocard circle (§§ 666–683), introducing Brocard’s first and second triangles and the Steiner and Tarry points.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities¶

  • §§ 666–675 — The Brocard Circle & Brocard Triangles: The circle on diameter \(OK\) passes through \(O, K, \Omega, \Omega'\) (with \(O\Omega = O\Omega'\), \(\angle\Omega O\Omega' = 2\omega\)), as well as the three vertices of Brocard’s first and second triangles!

  • §§ 676–683 — Steiner Point X(99) & Tarry Point X(98): Parallels through \(A, B, C\) to the sides of Brocard’s first triangle concur on the circumcircle at the Steiner point \(X(99)\), diametrically opposite the Tarry point \(X(98)\) where the corresponding perpendiculars concur.

Constructive & Symbolic Connections¶

Maps directly to Kimberling centers \(X(39)\), \(X(98)\), \(X(99)\), and \(X(182)\) in geometor/etc.

See Commentary on Chapter X: Recent Geometry of the Triangle and 334-Folio Archival Scan Inventory.
