Section E.a — Brocard Geometry: Brocard Points¶

Chapter X: Recent Geometry of the Triangle • Articles §§ 649–665 (Folios 0293–0298, pp. 274–279)

Introduces the asymmetric rotational jewel of triangle geometry (§§ 649–665): the direct and retrograde Brocard points \(\Omega, \Omega'\) and the Brocard angle \(\omega\) (Crelle 1816, Jacobi 1828, Brocard 1881).

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities¶

  • §§ 649–655 — Construction via Tangent Adjunct Circles: Three circles tangent to \(BC, CA, AB\) at \(B, C, A\) and passing through \(C, A, B\) concur at the first Brocard point \(\Omega\) where \(\angle\Omega BC = \angle\Omega CA = \angle\Omega AB = \omega\); cyclic reversal yields \(\Omega'\), the isogonal conjugate of \(\Omega\)!

  • §§ 656–665 — Brocard Angle Formula & Inequality: Proves \(\cot\omega = \cot A + \cot B + \cot C = (a^2 + b^2 + c^2) / 4S\), \(\csc^2\omega = \csc^2 A + \csc^2 B + \csc^2 C\), and \(\omega \le 30^\circ\) (equality iff \(\triangle ABC\) is equilateral).

Constructive & Symbolic Connections¶

Notice \(\cot\omega = (Q_a + Q_b + Q_c) / 4S\) is rational in the side quadrances and area, connecting Brocard geometry to geometor/etc (\(X(39)\) Brocard midpoint) and geometor/wildberger.
