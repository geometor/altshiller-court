Section A — Introductory (Directed Segments & Euler’s Identity)¶

Chapter VI: Transversals • Articles §§ 306–307 (Folios 0170–0171, pp. 151–152)

Introduces signed directed segments on an oriented line (§§ 306–307, Möbius, Chasles), eliminating case-splitting between internal and external points of division.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities¶

  • § 306 — Chasles’s Relation: For any three collinear points \(A, B, C\) in any order, \(AB + BC + CA = 0\) and \(AB = -BA\), uniquely encoding a point on \(AB\) by its signed ratio \(AC / CB\).

  • § 307 — Euler’s Four-Point Collinear Identity: For any four collinear points \(A, B, C, D\), signed segments satisfy \(AB \cdot CD + AC \cdot DB + AD \cdot BC = 0\) (Euler).

Constructive & Symbolic Connections¶

Corresponds to affine 1D coordinate differences in geometor/model and underpins signed cross-ratios in geometor/pappus.
