Abbreviations: A. Books & B. Periodicals¶

Historical and Bibliographical Notes • Archival Apparatus (Folios 0314–0315, pp. 295–296)

Keys the bibliographic abbreviations on Folios 0314–0315 (pp. 295–296) to the eight classical treatises and ten nineteenth/twentieth-century mathematical journals cited throughout Altshiller-Court’s historical notes.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities¶

  • Foundational Treatises: Chasles (Chl.), Coolidge (Cn.), Altshiller-Court Modern Pure Solid Geometry (Crt.), Catalan (Ech.), Gallatly (Gy.), Lachlan (LHr.), Petersen (Pt.), and Rouché & de Comberousse.

  • Problem & Research Periodicals: Bulletin des sciences mathématiques (Bull.), Proceedings of the Edinburgh Mathematical Society (Edinb.), Educational Times (ET.), Giornale di Matematiche (GAM.), Journal de mathématiques élémentaires (JME.), Mathesis (Mathesis), American Mathematical Monthly (Monthly), and Nouvelles Annales (NA.).

Constructive & Symbolic Connections¶
