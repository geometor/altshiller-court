Historical and Bibliographical Notes (§§ 4–697)¶

Historical and Bibliographical Notes • Archival Apparatus (Folios 0315–0325, pp. 296–306)

Presents Altshiller-Court’s article-by-article historical and bibliographic commentary spanning §§ 4–697 across Folios 0315–0325 (pp. 296–306), documenting priority, independent rediscoveries, and journal problem origins.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities¶

  • Antiquity to Early Modern (§§ 4–344): Attributions to Plato, Euclid, Archimedes, Apollonius, Heron, Menelaus, Ptolemy, Pappus, Viète, Desargues, Ceva, Newton, Stewart, and Euler.

  • Projective & Circle Renaissance (§§ 345–546): Priority histories of Carnot, Brianchon, Poncelet, Gergonne, Feuerbach, Steiner, Chasles, Peaucellier, and Casey.

  • Modern Triangle Geometry (§§ 547–697): Detailed journal citations for Lemoine, Brocard, Neuberg, d'Ocagne, Tucker, Taylor, Tarry, and Thébault.

Constructive & Symbolic Connections¶

Cross-linked prosopographically throughout this edition via Index of Persons and Systematic Bibliography.
