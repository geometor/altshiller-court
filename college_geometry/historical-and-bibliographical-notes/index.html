Historical and Bibliographical Notes¶

Books & Periodicals Key, Article-by-Article Historical Attributions (§§ 4–697), and Alphabetical List of Names

Altshiller-Court’s historiographical apparatus (Folios 0314–0328, pp. 295–309): abbreviations of foundational treatises and periodicals, article-by-article historical attributions spanning §§ 4–697, and the alphabetical List of Names.

Abbreviations: A. Books & B. Periodicals

Keys the bibliographic abbreviations on Folios 0314–0315 (pp. 295–296) to the eight classical treatises and ten nineteenth/twentieth-century mathematical journals cited throughout Altshiller-Court’s historical notes.

Historical and Bibliographical Notes (§§ 4–697)

Presents Altshiller-Court’s article-by-article historical and bibliographic commentary spanning §§ 4–697 across Folios 0315–0325 (pp. 296–306), documenting priority, independent rediscoveries, and journal problem origins.

List of Names

Alphabetical prosopographical index across Folios 0326–0328 (pp. 307–309) mapping every geometer named in College Geometry to page numbers in the text and historical notes.
