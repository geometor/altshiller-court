Section A — Inverse Points¶

Chapter VIII: Circles • Articles §§ 358–362 (Folios 0191–0193, pp. 172–174)

Defines inverse points \(P, P'\) with respect to a reference circle \((O, R)\) (§§ 358–362) via collinearity on a ray from \(O\) and \(OP \cdot OP' = R^2\).

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities¶

  • §§ 358–360 — Harmonic Diameter Separation: Two points \(P, P'\) on a diameter line are inverse with respect to \((O, R)\) if and only if they divide the endpoints of that diameter harmonically (§ 63).

  • §§ 361–362 — Ruler-and-Compass Construction & Ratio Property: Constructs \(P'\) via right-triangle chords or tangent secants, and shows that \((O, R)\) is an Apollonian circle for every pair of inverse points \(MP / MP' = OP / R\).

Constructive & Symbolic Connections¶

Establishes the point-pair foundation for poles/polars (§ 373) and conformal inversion (Chapter IX).
