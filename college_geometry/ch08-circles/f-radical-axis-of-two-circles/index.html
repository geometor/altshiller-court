Section F — Radical Axis of Two Circles¶

Chapter VIII: Circles • Articles §§ 421–439 (Folios 0213–0220, pp. 194–201)

Constructs and characterizes the radical axis of two non-concentric circles (§§ 421–439, Gaultier 1813, Steiner): the straight-line locus of points having equal powers with respect to both circles.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities¶

  • §§ 421–428 — Linear Locus & Common Secant/Tangent: Generalizes Chapter I Locus 12 (\(PO^2 - PO'^2 = R^2 - R'^2\)): perpendicular to the line of centers \(OO'\), coinciding with the common chord when circles intersect, common internal tangent when tangent, and bisecting all four common tangents.

  • §§ 429–439 — Construction for Non-Intersecting Circles: Introduces an auxiliary circle cutting both given circles, intersecting their two common chords at a point on the required radical axis, and proves that orthogonal circles have centers on the radical axis.

Constructive & Symbolic Connections¶

Subtracting two circle equations \((x - x_i)^2 + (y - y_i)^2 - R_i^2 = 0\) in geometor/model cancels \(x^2 + y^2\), producing the exact linear radical axis over \(\mathbb{Q}\).
