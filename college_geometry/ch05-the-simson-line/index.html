Chapter V: The Simson Line¶

Wallace-Simson Collinearity, Angle Properties, Orthocenter Bisection on the Nine-Point Circle

Pedal projections from the circumcircle onto the sidelines of a triangle (§§ 282–305, Folios 0159–0169, pp. 140–150): the Wallace–Simson collinearity criterion, bisection of the orthocenter segment on the nine-point circle, and angular intersection laws.

The Simson Line (Wallace-Simson Theorems & Exercises)

Develops the complete synthetic theory of the Wallace–Simson line (§§ 282–305, Folios 0159–0169, pp. 140–150), discovered by William Wallace in 1799 and traditionally named after Robert Simson .
