Section A — The General Quadrilateral¶

Chapter IV: The Quadrilateral • Articles §§ 240–250 (Folios 0143–0146, pp. 124–127)

Opens the 1952 Second Edition’s new Chapter IV (§§ 240–250) by developing affine and metric properties valid for any planar quadrilateral \(ABCD\)—convex, re-entrant, or crossed.

Principal Theorems, Constructions & Identities¶

  • §§ 240–244 — Varignon’s Parallelogram & Bimedians: The midpoints \(E, F, G, H\) of the four sides form Varignon's parallelogram whose sides are parallel to and half the diagonals \(AC, BD\), and whose area equals half the area of \(ABCD\); the two bimedians and the segment joining the diagonal midpoints bisect one another at the vertex centroid.

  • §§ 245–247 — Euler’s Quadrilateral Identity: The sum of the squares of the four sides equals the sum of the squares of the two diagonals plus four times the square of the segment joining the midpoints of the diagonals (Euler 1748).

  • §§ 248–250 — Area & Orthodiagonal Characterization: Area via \(\tfrac{1}{2} AC \cdot BD \sin\theta\), and proof that \(AB^2 + CD^2 = BC^2 + DA^2\) holds if and only if \(AC \perp BD\).

Constructive & Symbolic Connections¶

Modeled with exact integer coordinates in the cyclic_quadrilateral hero diagram, verifying parallelism of opposite Varignon sides symbolically.
