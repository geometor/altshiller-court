§ 22. Exercises (1–4)¶

[Folio 0047 | p. 28]

Construct a triangle, given:

  1. \(h_c - h_b, B, C\).

  2. \(h_c - h_b, b, c\).

  3. \(h_c - h_b, b - c, B - C\).

  4. \(h_c - h_b, A, b + c\).

See Commentary on § 22. Exercises (1–4).
