Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

Section 22 is the exact subtractive counterpart to § 20: laying off \(FG = BE = h_b\) along \(CF = h_c\) isolates \(CG = h_c - h_b\) inside right triangle \(\triangle CGH\) on hypotenuse \(CH = b - c\) opposite \(\angle CHG = A\), embodying the relation \(h_c - h_b = (b - c)\sin A\). Notice the printed alternate vertex order CHG in the penultimate line (‘from the triangle CHG’), preserved diplomatically and resolved seamlessly via polygon permutation indexing.

Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

Notice in Exercise 4 (\(h_c - h_b, A, b + c\)) how the two data of §§ 21 and 22 interlock: from \((h_c - h_b, A)\) the subtractive datum yields \(b - c\), which combined with \(b + c\) immediately determines the individual sides \(b\) and \(c\) together with the included angle \(A\)! Supplementary Exercises 9–11 form a classic secant-through-circle-intersection suite solved by projecting the line of centers onto the variable secant (constructing a right triangle on the center-segment \(OO'\) whose leg equals half the chord-sum, chord-difference, or chord-ratio projection).
