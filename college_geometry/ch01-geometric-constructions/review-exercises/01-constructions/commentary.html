Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

Supplementary Exercise 15 reduces the perimeter of trapezoid \(BB'C'C\) to an indirect sum along the sidelines \(AB, AC\), while Exercise 16 constructs a triangle whose sides are internally divided in given ratios by three fixed points (a classic affine-dilation / parallel-translation problem). In REVIEW EXERCISES — CONSTRUCTIONS, items 1–13 synthesize all four pillars of Chapter I: translation of segments (item 7), capable-arc loci (items 1, 7), tangent-envelope circles for fixed chord lengths (items 2, 12), and positional circumcircle/incircle constraints (items 8–10).

Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

Item 17 (\(a - b, h_b + h_c, A\)) pairs the datum \(\{b + c, h_b + h_c, A\}\) of § 21 (yielding \(b + c\)) with \(a - b\) to give \(a + c = (a - b) + (b + c)\), reducing the triangle to two linear conditions and an angle. In PROPOSITIONS, item 29 states van Schooten’s theorem (Ptolemy’s theorem applied to an equilateral triangle inscribed in a circle: \(PA + PB = PC\) when \(P\) lies on minor arc \(AB\)), and item 30 establishes the area doubling of the parallel-cevian reflection/Steiner-type triangle \(XYZ\).
