Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

Note two signature conventions of Altshiller-Court (§ 1): (1) \(2p\) denotes the perimeter so that \(p\) is the semiperimeter (where many British texts write \(2s\) and \(s\)); (2) \((A, r)\) and \(M = (PQ, RS)\) correspond directly to model.construct_circle_by_center_and_point and line-line intersection nodes in geometor.model. The three § 2 items on this page invoke Euclid VI.9, VI.10, and VI.12.
