Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

In § 5, \(O\) and \(O'\) are harmonic conjugates with respect to \(A\) and \(B\) (anticipating Chapter II, § 59). When \(p = q\), the internal division point \(O'\) is the midpoint of \(AB\), while the external division point \(O\) recedes to infinity along \(AB\), so the second solution becomes the line through \(C\) parallel to \(AB\)!

Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

Problem 7 is a classic gem of synthetic analysis (also solvable by homothecy in Chapter II): by projecting the unknown broken equal chain \(AD = DE = EC\) from \(A\) onto \(B\), the scale ambiguity vanishes and \(K, L\) are constructed by two compass transfers of \(BA\). When \(\angle A = 90^\circ\), \(K\) coincides with \(A\).
