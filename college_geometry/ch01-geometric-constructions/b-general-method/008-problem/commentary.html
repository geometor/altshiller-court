Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

Problem 8 exemplifies Julius Petersen's Method of Parallel Translation (Methods and Theories): translating circle \((A)\) by \(\pm\vec{m}\) yields two circles centered at \(R, R'\) whose intersections with \((B)\) give up to four solutions \(P_1Q_1, P_2Q_2, P_1'Q_1', P_2'Q_2'\) (Fig. 5).
