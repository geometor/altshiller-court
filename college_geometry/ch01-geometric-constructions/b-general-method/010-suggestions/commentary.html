Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

The poristic case in the Discussion (\(F = B\), when \(AC \perp BD\) and \(AC = BD\), an orthodiagonal equidiagonal quadruple) yields a 1-parameter infinity of circumscribed squares—a striking preview of Chapter IV (Other Quadrilaterals, §§ 271–282).
