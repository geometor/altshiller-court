Chapter I: Geometric Constructions — Commentary¶

Constructive Analysis, Historical Provenance & GEOMETOR Notes (0020–0052, pp. 1–33)

Constructive Architecture & Symbolic Modeling (§§ 1–22)¶

Chapter I lays down the operational grammar of the entire book. Every ruler-and-compass construction is governed by Pappus' four-stage analytic method (Collection, Book VII):

  1. Analysis — Assume the required configuration already exists and trace algebraic/synthetic incidences back to known data.

  2. Construction — Execute elementary straightedge (line(P, Q)) and compass (circle(A, r)) steps in forward topological order.

  3. Proof — Verify deductively that the constructed object satisfies every hypothesis.

  4. Discussion (Diorismos) — Determine exact existence inequalities (s < 2 OE, s^2 > 2a^2), count multiplicity of solutions (0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, or \(\infty\)), and identify poristic degeneracies (as in § 9 when \(AC \perp BD\) and \(AC = BD\)).

Mapping Chapter I to geometor.model & geometor.elements¶

  • Symbolic DAG Representation: Altshiller-Court’s § 1 notation—circles \((A, r)\) and line intersections \(M = (PQ, RS)\)—maps one-to-one onto geometor.model primitives, where every constructed point retains its exact SymPy algebraic coordinates in a quadratic extension tower of \(\mathbb{Q}\).

  • The Twelve Fundamental Loci (§ 11, Folios `0030`–`0036`): - Linear Loci: Parallel pair (Locus 3), mediator (Locus 4, Euclid I.10–11), angle bisectors (Locus 5), and constant difference of squares \(AM^2 - BM^2 = d^2\) (Locus 12 — the point-circle precursor of the radical axis in Chapter VIII, § 422). - Circular Loci: Circle \((A, r)\) (Locus 1), concentric tangent-length & subtended-angle circles (Loci 2, 6, 9), capable arc & Thales’ circle (Locus 7, Euclid III.21, III.31), chord-midpoint circle on \(OP\) (Locus 8), Apollonius’ median circle \(OM^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}s^2 - a^2\) (Locus 10), and the Apollonian circle \(AM : MB = p : q\) (Locus 11, Apollonius).

  • Transformational & Unfolding Previews: § 8 previews parallel translation (Petersen), § 9 previews \(90^\circ\) rotational congruence (Castillon), and § 15 introduces indirect elements by unfolding perimeter \(2p\) into auxiliary triangle \(AEF\) with vertex angle \(90^\circ + \tfrac{1}{2}A\).
