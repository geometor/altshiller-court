Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

In § 15, unfolding \(BA\) and \(CA\) onto line \(BC\) turns the indirect perimeter \(2p\) into the single segment \(EF = 2p\) of auxiliary triangle \(AEF\), which is constructed from base \(EF = 2p\), capable arc for \(\angle EAF = 90^\circ + \frac{1}{2}A\) (Locus 7), and parallel line at height \(h_a\) (Locus 3); on folio 0041 (p. 22), vertices \(B, C\) are recovered as the traces on \(EF\) of the mediators of \(AE\) and \(AF\)!
