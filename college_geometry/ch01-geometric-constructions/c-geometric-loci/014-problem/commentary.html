Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

Raw OCR misread 2 OE as 2 OR and FIG. 15 as FIG. 16; verified against 0038.png. Note the topological transition at \(s = EG\) in § 13: when \(s < EG\), the parallel chords cross inside the circle so that \(2\,EF = |AC - BD|\) instead of \(AC + BD\), immediately solving the companion difference problem!
