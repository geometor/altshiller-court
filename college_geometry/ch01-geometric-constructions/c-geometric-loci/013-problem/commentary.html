Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

In § 12, notice that \(O\) also lies on the mediator of \(AB\), which is simultaneously the radical axis of the two Apollonian circles \((EF)\) and \((GH)\)—giving at most two centers \(O, O'\)! In § 13, Locus 9 and Locus 1 intersect cleanly at \(F\).

Mathematical & Constructive Commentary¶

Note the topological transition at \(s = EG\) in § 13: when \(s < EG\), the parallel chords cross inside the circle so that \(2\,EF = |AC - BD|\) instead of \(AC + BD\), immediately solving the companion difference problem!
